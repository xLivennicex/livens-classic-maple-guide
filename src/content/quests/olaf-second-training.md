  <h3>What Return Scroll (Nearest Town) does</h3>
  <p>Instant teleport to your nearest town from any map.
  At L8 in Lith Harbor, "nearest town" is typically Lith
  itself. Later, as you travel deeper into Victoria,
  the scroll targets whichever town is geographically
  closest.</p>

  <p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> at L8 you don't own
  destination-specific Return Scrolls yet (those come
  later from
  <a href="/quests/shumis-request">Shumi's Request</a> at
  L10 and beyond). Nearest Town scrolls are the fallback
  when you're deep in a mob zone and need to get out
  fast.</p>
