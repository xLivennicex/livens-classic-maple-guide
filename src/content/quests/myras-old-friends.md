editorial: |
  <p>Myra's Old Friends is <strong>the emotional grief arc
  of Forgotten Hollow</strong>. Where Nyroth's chain
  reveals the mechanics of the corruption (Arcforging
  fails against malice), Myra's chain reveals the
  <em>human cost</em> - her companions have been changed
  by the same corruption.</p>

  <h3>The 2 quests</h3>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>10603 - Old Friends.</strong> Myra is
      grief-stricken. Her companions in Perilous Descent I
      have "changed" (mechanically: they've become corrupted
      mob variants). Put them to rest. EXP + mesos only,
      no item reward - the mercy IS the reward.</li>
    <li><strong>10604 - Myra's Old Friend.</strong> Deliver
      word to Grendel the Really Old (Ellinia) about what
      happened. Rewards 15 Mana Elixirs.</li>
  </ol>

  <h3>Character web reveal: Myra + Grendel</h3>
  <p>Myra is explicitly named as Grendel's "old friend."
  This retroactively upgrades
  <a href="/quests/welcome-to-the-hollow">Grendel's L39
  Hollow arc</a>:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Grendel was searching for his fairy kin (revealed
      L39)</li>
    <li>Myra is one of his old friends who lived among
      the fairies (revealed L49)</li>
    <li>Myra survived; her companions did not</li>
    <li>Grendel now learns the fate of his people through
      you as messenger</li>
  </ul>

  <p><strong>The narrative weight:</strong> Myra IS Grendel's
  emotional link to the Hollow. Delivering her message is
  YOU being the bridge between two old friends separated
  by tragedy. Classic World writers earned this arc.</p>

  <h3>Where Perilous Descent I is</h3>
  <p>Perilous Descent I is a Forgotten Hollow sub-zone -
  deeper than the entry Hollow map. Access via The Tree
  Tunnel (Ellinia) -> Forgotten Hollow -> Perilous
  Descent I. L45-50 mob density. Solo-viable at L49.</p>
