editorial: |
  <p>Huckle's quest is <strong>your first cross-continent
  quest</strong>. Ellinia has a portal to Orbis (via a
  bird-boat ferry from the Ellinia tree platforms).
  Orbis is the entry to the <strong>Ossyria continent</strong>
  - a whole second-half of the game with its own towns,
  mobs, quests, and job progression paths.</p>

  <h3>Where Orbis Tower is</h3>
  <p>Ellinia -> Orbis ferry (bird-boat, ~5 minute ride) ->
  Orbis Tower. Enter the tower and navigate to the middle-
  floor hidden secret room. Huckle stands inside, exactly
  where he's stood for 30 years.</p>

  <h3>Character context: Huckle</h3>
  <p>Huckle is a Physics-of-Transformation researcher. What
  that means:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>He studies how ordinary objects can transform (e.g.,
      pumpkin into carriage, straw into gold, etc.).</li>
    <li>His 30-year isolation is a running Orbis joke -
      Orbis NPCs occasionally reference "the crazy old man
      in the tower" without explaining.</li>
    <li>His research produces Orbis Rock Scrolls (which
      you receive as reward). These Scrolls likely feed
      into higher-tier Orbis crafting.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Ingredients requested</h3>
  <p>The datamine truncates the exact requirements, but
  based on quest patterns and Huckle's research theme, expect
  drops from Orbis Tower mobs (bird-family mobs, likely
  L27-30). Since you're at L29 already, the fights are
  in-range.</p>

  <h3>Strategic timing</h3>
  <p><strong>Do this AFTER your job advancement (L30).</strong>
  Huckle's quest is accepted at L29 but the completion
  requires venturing deeper into Orbis - safer as a
  first-job-advanced character. If you accept at L29 and
  can't complete, hold the quest until you're L30+ then
  finish.</p>
