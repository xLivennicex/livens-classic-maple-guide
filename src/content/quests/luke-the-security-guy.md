  <p><strong>Which gem is best?</strong> Depends on your
  needs:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Diamond</strong> is the highest-FM-value
      gem and the input for two flagship L27+ quest chains.
      Best to roll.</li>
    <li><strong>Garnet</strong> is second-best - it's the
      Estelle prerequisite, so if you haven't done that chain
      yet, a Garnet skips your grinding step.</li>
    <li><strong>The other 6 gems</strong> are effectively
      interchangeable for FM sale (~2,000-5,000 mesos each
      depending on server prices).</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Where the mobs are</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Jr. Neckis:</strong> Henesys Dungeon
      (accessed via the same entrance Luke guards). L20
      snake-family mob.</li>
    <li><strong>Stirges:</strong> Henesys Dungeon deeper
      section. L21 flying mob - can be annoying for melee
      classes without a jump attack.</li>
  </ul>
