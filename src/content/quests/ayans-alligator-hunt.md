  <h3>The Perion Tribes reveal</h3>
  <p>Ayan's mention of "the tribespeople of Perion" is a
  worldbuilding drop. Perion is Classic MapleStory's
  Warrior town, and its citizens have consistently been
  described as tribal / clan-based (see Manji, Blackbull,
  Aya). Alligators overrunning the swamp is a real
  external threat to Perion's cultural continuity - not
  just a fetch-quest excuse.</p>

  <h3>Where the Alligators are</h3>
  <p>Alligators (Ligators, technically) live in the Perion
  swamp / Kerning subway zone (they migrate between the
  two). At L48 you're comfortably over-leveled - expect
  fast kills and low potion consumption.</p>
