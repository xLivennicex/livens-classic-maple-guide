  <h3>THE Pet Equipment Scroll reveal - NEW REWARD CATEGORY</h3>
  <p>This is <strong>Classic MapleStory's earliest documented
  Pet Equipment Scroll source</strong>. Pet Equipment
  Scrolls target pet accessories:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Pet collars</strong> - stat-boosting neck
      accessories for your pet</li>
    <li><strong>Pet harnesses</strong> - defensive gear</li>
    <li><strong>Pet ornaments</strong> - cosmetic + utility</li>
  </ul>
  <p><strong>The 4-scroll pool:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>Speed Scrolls (Int/Greater) - increase pet movement
      speed</li>
    <li>Jump Scrolls (Int/Greater) - increase pet jump height</li>
  </ul>
  <p><strong>Pet mechanics:</strong> pets in MapleStory
  follow you around. Faster pets keep up better; higher-
  jump pets reach vertical platforms with you. Pet
  Equipment Scrolls are quality-of-life upgrades for
  players who use pets extensively (auto-loot, follow,
  cosmetic display).</p>

  <h3>Cross-reference: Mar the Fairy</h3>
  <p>Pet Equipment Scrolls are only useful if your pet is
  ALIVE. If your pet has expired, visit
  <a href="/quests/mar-fairy-water-of-life">Mar the Fairy
  in Ellinia</a> to revive it with the Water of Life.</p>

  <h3>Where to acquire pets in the first place</h3>
  <p>Pets in Classic MapleStory typically come from:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Cash Shop premium purchases</li>
    <li>Rare event drops (7-day expiring pets)</li>
    <li>Special quest rewards (rare)</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Pet Equipment Scrolls have zero utility if you don't
  own a pet - FM sell to Cash Shop pet owners for 3,000-
