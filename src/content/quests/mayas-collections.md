  <h3>The 2 collection quests</h3>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>10115 - Maya's First Collection.</strong>
      20 each of: Orange Mushroom Cap, Green Mushroom Cap,
      Blue Mushroom Cap, Horny Mushroom Cap. Rewards 20
      Orange Potions.</li>
    <li><strong>10116 - Maya's Second Collection.</strong>
      20 each of: Pig's Ribbon, Bubbling's Huge Bubble, Fire
      Boar's Tooth, Tablecloth. Rewards 20 Blue Potions.</li>
  </ol>

    <li><strong>Green Mushroom Cap:</strong> Ellinia tree
      platforms.</li>
    <li><strong>Blue Mushroom Cap:</strong> Perion Rocky
      Mountains + Sleepywood approach.</li>
    <li><strong>Horny Mushroom Cap:</strong> Sleepywood
      dungeon entrance zones.</li>
  </ul>

  <ul>
    <li><strong>Pig's Ribbon:</strong> Henesys prairie Ribbon

    <li><strong>Bubbling's Huge Bubble:</strong> Sleepywood

  </ul>
