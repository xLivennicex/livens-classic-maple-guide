editorial: |
  <p>Blackbull's quest is Perion's biggest L11 grind - 40 total
  Stumps + 10 Tree Branches. The upside: unlike most L10-11
  quests that reward consumables, Blackbull hands you a
  permanent equipment upgrade.</p>

  <p><strong>Where:</strong> Blackbull is in Perion's central
  area. Stumps and Dark Stumps live on the west and north
  exits respectively. Tree Branches drop from both mob types
  as an ETC drop.</p>

  <p><strong>Shield mechanics:</strong> shields occupy the
  off-hand slot on classes that CAN wear one (primarily
  Warriors, some Magician sub-classes with Wands). Bowmen
  and Thieves use two-handed or dual-wield weapons and
  can't equip a shield. If you're not a Warrior, the shield
  reward is either a bank item, an NPC sell, or a Free Market
  trade candidate.</p>

  <p><strong>Wooden Buckler vs Steel Shield:</strong> Steel
  Shield is the strictly better roll - higher base defense,
  slightly higher level requirement, more upgrade slots.
  Neither is a permanent keeper by mid-20s, but both bridge
  the gap until you find a scrolled shield in Free Market
  or drop one from a Perion-area mob.</p>
