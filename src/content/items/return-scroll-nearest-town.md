# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Return Scrolls consume one use to instantly teleport your
  character to a town. The generic "Nearest Town" variant is
  the most flexible - it picks the town closest to wherever
  you are, which is usually the town you'd want to visit
  anyway.</p>

  <p><strong>Why this is essential:</strong> without Return
  Scrolls, every training session ends with a long walk (or
  a taxi mesos fee) back to town. Multiply that by 10-20
  sessions per day and Return Scrolls save literal hours of
  play time per week.</p>

  <p><strong>Town-specific variants:</strong> Return Scrolls
  also exist for each specific town:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Return Scroll to Lith Harbor (2030001)</li>
    <li>Return Scroll to Ellinia (2030002)</li>
    <li>Return Scroll to Perion (2030003)</li>
    <li>Return Scroll to Henesys (2030004)</li>
    <li>Return Scroll to Kerning City (2030005)</li>
    <li>Return Scroll to Sleepywood (2030006)</li>
  </ul>
  <p>The town-specific variants are useful when you want to
  hop somewhere OTHER than the nearest town - e.g., you're
  training near Sleepywood but need to visit Henesys for a
  Free Market run.</p>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong> most towns sell them at
  the general item shop for a modest meso cost (typically
  1,000-2,000 mesos in v83, likely similar in Classic World).
  Buy in bulk - a stack of 100 costs less than one training
  session's mesos, and lasts weeks.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Muscle memory:</strong> most players end up
  binding Return Scrolls to a hotkey. Popular choice: same
  hotkey slot as your primary attack, so you use it
  reflexively when you decide a training session is over.
