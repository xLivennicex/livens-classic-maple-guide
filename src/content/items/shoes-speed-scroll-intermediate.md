# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Intermediate tier upgrade scroll for the Shoes slot. When
  applied to any shoes, rolls a <strong>60% success chance</strong>;
  on success, adds <strong>+2 Speed</strong> permanently to
  those shoes. On failure, the scroll is consumed and one of
  the shoes' upgrade slots is spent (with a small "boom" chance
  destroying the item outright).</p>

  <p><strong>Why Speed matters:</strong> Movement Speed
  affects walk speed AND jump distance. Every +2 stacks means
  you cross maps faster, retreat from bad situations faster,
  and loop training routes more efficiently. Over hundreds of
  hours of play, a well-scrolled shoe with +8-10 Speed pays
  back its scroll investment many times over in saved
  minutes.</p>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong> the primary source is
  <a href="/quests/ronnies-house-building-chain">Ronnie's
  House-Building chain</a> final step (guaranteed - one
  scroll per full chain completion). Also drops occasionally
  from mid-tier mobs and is sold on the Free Market.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> everyone. Movement
  Speed has no downside for any class. Warriors close the
  distance to mobs faster; Bowmen kite more effectively;
  Thieves position for backstabs faster; Magicians reposition
  between casts more freely.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Speed cap awareness:</strong> Classic MapleStory
  caps character Movement Speed at 140% (base is 100%). If
  your shoes already push you near the cap, additional Speed
  scrolls may hit diminishing returns. Check your Speed
  stat before committing your last scroll slot.
