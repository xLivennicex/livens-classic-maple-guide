# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Diamond is an ETC-category gemstone that drops rarely from
  mid-to-high-level mobs. In-game description: "A transparent
  jewel." No stats, no equip requirement - it's a pure crafting
  and quest material.</p>

  <p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> Diamond is the bottleneck
  item for two of Classic MapleStory's iconic quest chains:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <a href="/quests/sleepywood-sauna-robe-chain">Sleepywood
      Sauna Robe chain</a> step 6 - Rowen the Fairy trades a
      Diamond for the Fresh Milk needed to complete the chain.
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="/quests/ronnies-house-building-chain">Ronnie's
      House-Building chain</a> step 4 (final) - 1 Diamond is
      required alongside 30 Cold Eye Tails and 10 Dragon Skins.
    </li>
  </ul>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong> Diamond drops from
  various mid-level mobs (Zombie Lupins near Ellinia, Cold
  Drakes in Ice Valley, various Sleepywood mobs). Drop rate
  is low - historical v83 estimates put it around 0.5-2%
  depending on mob.</p>

  <p><strong>Diamond vs Diamond Ore:</strong> two different
  items! <em>Diamond Ore</em> (WZ ID 4020007) is a crafting
  ore that drops from the
  <a href="/quests/cursed-doll-chain">Cursed Doll chain</a>
  tier 3 - it's a smithing input. <em>Diamond</em> (this page,
  WZ ID 4020107) is the finished gem used as a trade / quest
  input. If a quest asks for a Diamond and your inventory
  has Diamond Ore, you can't substitute - they're distinct.</p>

  <p><strong>Free Market strategy:</strong> Diamonds trade for
  mid-hundreds-of-thousands of mesos in v83 economies during
  active quest completion phases. If you need one for a
  chain and don't want to grind, buying is often faster than
  farming.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Bank at least one.</strong> If you're grinding
  Zombie Lupins for the Cursed Doll chain or Cold Drakes for
  training and a Diamond drops - keep it. You WILL need it
  later for the Sleepywood or Ronnie House chains. Selling
  early is a classic newbie mistake.
