# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Intermediate tier upgrade scroll. When applied to any
  Earring, rolls a <strong>60% success chance</strong>; on
  success, adds <strong>+2 LUK</strong> permanently to that
  earring. On failure, the scroll is consumed and one of the
  earring's upgrade slots is spent (with a small "boom" chance
  destroying the earring outright).</p>

  <p>Classic World's <a href="/quests/first-time-together">Kerning Party Quest</a>
  reward table includes this as one of four random earring
  scroll outcomes. In a Thief-heavy party you'll be first in
  line whenever LUK rolls; in mixed parties expect to trade for
  or buy them off other members.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> Thieves specifically.
  Lucky Seven's damage formula is <em>entirely</em> LUK-scaling
  (weapon attack is irrelevant), which means every point of LUK
  is directly a point of damage. +10 LUK from stacking these
  scrolls is one of the single largest per-piece damage
  upgrades available in the level 20-40 range.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Assassin math:</strong> Critical Throw at max level
  adds a 40% crit chance to your Lucky Sevens, with crits
  landing at 1.4x damage. Since Lucky Seven's damage IS your
  LUK stat, +10 LUK from earring scrolls = +40 damage per
  normal hit and +56 per crit. For a class that fires 2 Lucky
  Sevens per attack cycle, this compounds fast.
