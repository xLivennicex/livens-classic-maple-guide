# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Intermediate tier upgrade scroll. When applied to any
  Earring, rolls a <strong>60% success chance</strong>; on
  success, adds <strong>+2 INT</strong> permanently to that
  earring. On failure, the scroll is consumed and one of the
  earring's upgrade slots is spent (with a small "boom" chance
  destroying the earring outright).</p>

  <p>Classic World's <a href="/quests/first-time-together">Kerning Party Quest</a>
  drops one of the four earring stat scrolls per completion, so
  every run has a 25% chance of yielding this one specifically.
  Magician mains typically stockpile mesos to buy INT rolls off
  party members who don't need them.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> Magicians of all three
  second-job branches (F/P, I/L, Cleric all scale off INT for
  damage AND max MP). Occasionally useful for hybrid stat
  builds, but the DEX and LUK variants have wider audience
  overlap.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Magician earring math:</strong> INT gives ~14 MP per
  point at low levels, so +10 INT from 5 scroll successes is
  ~140 extra MP on top of the direct damage boost. Magicians
  benefit from these scrolls doubly - and Magic Guard means
  more MP is more effective HP.
