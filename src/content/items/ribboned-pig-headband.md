# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>The Ribboned Pig Headband is the reward for completing
  <a href="/quests/estelles-special-sauce">Estelle's Special Sauce</a>,
  the classic Henesys sauce-delivery chain. Mechanically it's a
  respectable mid-game hat: <strong>+26 Weapon Defense</strong>,
  <strong>+7 MaxMP</strong>, <strong>+2 Accuracy</strong>, and 7
  upgrade slots at a <strong>level 27 requirement</strong>. As a
  fashion piece it's been iconic since v83. If you see a level
  27-40 character with a pink pigtail-ribbon hat, they did the
  sauce quest.</p>
  <p>The item slot for the Ribboned Pig Headband is Hat, so it
  competes with your normal defensive headwear. That's the
  tradeoff: this hat is worn for the aesthetic (and for the
  memory of the quest), not for its stat sheet. If you're
  building for pure combat efficiency, keep your Pilgrim's Hat
  or Bamboo Hat.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Save the box.</strong> The Ribboned Pig Headband is not
  purchasable and, unlike most low-level cosmetics, isn't a
  seasonal reissue. If you delete or NPC it, the only way to
  get another one is to reroll a character and redo the sauce
  chain. Bank it.
