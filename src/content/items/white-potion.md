# ==== Editorial - our own flavor ====
editorial: |
  <p>The White Potion is the workhorse HP recovery item of the 20-40
  level range. Restores a big chunk of HP per pop, cheap enough to
  buy in stacks of 100 without flinching, and sold by almost every
  town's general potion vendor. If you're a Warrior training past
  level 25, you'll go through hundreds of these per grinding session -
  it's basically part of the training cost budget.</p>
  <p>Magicians typically skip White Potions in favor of Mana potions
  (thanks to Magic Guard converting damage-to-MP - see the
  <a href="/jobs/magician">Magician guide</a>). Everyone else is
  buying these by the stack.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Restore value note:</strong> the exact HP-restore number
  in Classic World is one of the values we're waiting to verify at
  Founder's Access. v83 baseline was around 1,000 HP per potion,
  but Nexon has been known to rebalance consumables between versions.
