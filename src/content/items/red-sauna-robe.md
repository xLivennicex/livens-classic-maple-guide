# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>The Red Sauna Robe is the female-character reward for the
  <a href="/quests/sleepywood-sauna-robe-chain">Sleepywood
  Sauna Robe Chain</a>. Mr. Wetbottom's payoff is
  gender-locked at award time: female characters receive this
  Red variant, male characters receive the
  <a href="/items/blue-sauna-robe">Blue Sauna Robe</a>. You
  don't get to choose - the quest picks based on your
  character's gender.</p>

  <p><strong>Stats are IDENTICAL to the Blue variant:</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>+1 STR, +1 DEX, +1 INT, +1 LUK (universal-class flat +1)</li>
    <li>+75 Weapon Defense</li>
    <li>+10 MaxHP, +10 MaxMP</li>
    <li>10 upgrade slots (generous compared to typical L30 Overalls)</li>
    <li>1.5x HP/MP recovery multiplier when worn inside the Sleepywood VIP Sauna map</li>
  </ul>
  Mechanically these are literally the same item with a
  different sprite and a female-gender equip lock. All the
  strategic advice on the Blue variant's page - well-scrolled
  Overall Slots, universal-stat flexibility, pre-L50 gearing
  value - applies unchanged.</p>

  <p><strong>The sprite:</strong> a red-and-white striped
  bath towel with a red bow - the sauna aesthetic is on-brand.
  The Blue variant is the same silhouette in blue-and-white,
  same bow shape but blue. Both sprites come from MeowDB's
  CoT-accurate icon CDN.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Special mechanic:</strong> the 1.5x recovery bonus
  triggers ONLY inside the Sleepywood VIP Sauna map (where
  the chain begins). This is a cute thematic detail - the
  robe you earned for helping Mr. Wetbottom rewards you when
  you return to visit him. Mechanically minor (natural regen
  is slow at any multiplier), but a nice bit of world-building.
