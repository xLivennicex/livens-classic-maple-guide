# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>The Blue Sauna Robe is the reward for completing the
  <a href="/quests/sleepywood-sauna-robe-chain">Sleepywood
  Sauna Robe Chain</a>, a 9-step cross-continental fetch
  quest that starts in Sleepywood's VIP Sauna. Male characters
  receive this Blue variant; female characters receive the
  visually distinct but statistically identical
  <a href="/items/red-sauna-robe">Red Sauna Robe (1051004)</a> -
  the reward is gender-locked at award time. The sprite
  really does look like a striped bath towel with a bow.
  Sauna-appropriate.</p>

  <p><strong>Why the stats matter:</strong> Overalls occupy
  both the top and bottom body slots (replacing a shirt +
  pants combo). Getting a well-rolled Overall at level 30 means
  you don't need to buy or scroll two separate pieces - one
  slot, one investment, both defensive coverage AND stat
  bonuses.</p>

  <p><strong>The +1 to every stat is unusual.</strong> Most
  Overalls specialize in one stat. The Sauna Robe's flat +1
  everything makes it universally useful: a Warrior gets +1
  STR, a Magician gets +1 INT, a Bowman gets +1 DEX, a Thief
  gets +1 LUK. It also opens up hybrid builds - a Fighter
  investing in DEX for accuracy, a Bandit stacking STR for
  weapon requirements - because the "wrong" stat isn't
  wasted.</p>

  <p><strong>10 upgrade slots is generous.</strong> Most L30
  Overalls carry 7-8 slots. Ten slots means more room for
  60% Overall scrolls (STR / DEX / INT / LUK variants) to
  compound the base stats. A well-scrolled Sauna Robe can
  carry a character from 30 all the way into the mid-50s
  before an upgrade becomes attractive.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Special mechanic:</strong> when worn inside the
  Sleepywood VIP Sauna map (where the chain begins), the
  Sauna Robe boosts natural HP and MP recovery by 1.5x.
  Mechanically minor (regen is slow at any multiplier), but a
  clever thematic detail linking the reward back to its
  origin. Great for downtime AFK sessions if you happen to
  live near the sauna map.
