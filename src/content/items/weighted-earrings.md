  from <a href="/quests/mothers-gold-watch">Mother's Gold Watch</a>,
  the final step of the Alex/Chief Stan reconciliation chain.
  The drop is one of two possible outcomes (the other being
  Yellow Square) - random, one earring per completion. Also
  drops rarely from a handful of level 20-30 mobs.</p>

  <p><strong>Investment target:</strong> if you're planning to
  farm <a href="/quests/first-time-together">KPQ</a> starting
  at level 21, running the Alex chain FIRST at level 18 gives

  timing is intentional - Classic World's reward flow assumes
  this ordering.</p>

  niche crafting-bonus stat). Weighted is the recommended
  pick for 95% of builds - if you get the Yellow Square
  instead, either equip it and forget about the stat
  difference, or hope a friend rolled the opposite.
