editorial: |
  <p>Red Potion is Classic MapleStory's entry-tier HP
  consumable. Restores <strong>100 HP</strong> per use, no
  cooldown, 5 mesos to buy from any starter town's potion
  shop. It's the mainline HP potion for characters L1-30.</p>

  <p><strong>Why 100 HP matters:</strong> at level 1-10 a
  Warrior has ~120 HP total, so one Red Potion is nearly a
  full heal. Even at level 20-25 (~350 HP), a Red Potion
  restores roughly a third. Above L30, White Potion (300 HP
  restore) becomes the better ratio; Red Potion stays useful
  as low-cost inventory filler for training runs.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Warriors</strong> live and die by HP potions -
      Sword / Axe classes take hits by design. Keep 50-100
      Red Potions on hotkey slot 4.</li>
    <li><strong>Bowmen and Thieves</strong> keep smaller
      stacks (~20-30) for emergencies when kite-and-shoot
      goes wrong.</li>
    <li><strong>Magicians</strong> mostly use Magic Guard
      (converts damage to MP loss) so they need Blue Potion
      more than Red - but a small Red Potion stack is still
      useful when Magic Guard drops or you're out of MP.</li>
  </ul></p>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong> every starter town's
  potion shop sells Red Potions for 5 mesos each. Also drops
  from many low-level mobs. Also awarded by numerous L1-15
  quests: <a href="/quests/mushroom-studies-bruce">Bruce's
  Mushroom Studies</a> (10 potions), <a href="/quests/im-bored-2">Icarus's
  I'm Bored 2</a> (10 potions), <a href="/quests/to-henesys-the-prairie-town">To
  Henesys, the Prairie Town</a> (5 potions).</p>
