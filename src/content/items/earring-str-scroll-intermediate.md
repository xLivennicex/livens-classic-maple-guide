# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Intermediate tier upgrade scroll. When applied to any Earring
  slot item, rolls a <strong>60% success chance</strong>; on
  success, adds <strong>+2 STR</strong> to that earring
  permanently. On failure, the scroll is consumed and one of the
  earring's upgrade slots is spent. On a "boom" (10% chance on
  failure - <em>not</em> at the same time as the 60% success
  roll), the earring is destroyed.</p>

  <p>In v83 these scrolls were world-drop only, hoarded and
  traded on the Free Market for six-figure meso sums. Classic
  World bakes them into the <a href="/quests/first-time-together">Kerning Party Quest</a>
  reward table (as one of four possible drops, alongside DEX,
  INT, and LUK variants). Running KPQ regularly at levels
  21-30 is the intended acquisition path.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> primarily Warriors
  (STR is your damage stat). Bandits sometimes stack a few for
  the STR requirement to equip higher-tier daggers. Bowmen,
  Magicians, and Assassins get more value from the DEX / INT /
  LUK variants respectively.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Scroll stacking math:</strong> a Level 30 earring has
  5 upgrade slots. If you land all 5 as +2 STR successes, that
  earring alone gives +10 STR - enough to swing your damage
  bracket noticeably. Realistically, at 60% success you'll land
  ~3 of 5 on average, but the ceiling is what makes these
  scrolls the standout mid-game upgrade.
