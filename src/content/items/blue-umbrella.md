# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>The umbrella-as-sword line is one of MapleStory's oldest inside
  jokes. Wizet added a family of "umbrella swords" to the 1H-sword
  category as gag items - fully functional weapons that swing like
  swords in combat but look, well, like you're smacking mobs with
  a parasol.</p>
  <p><strong>The cross-class trick:</strong> because the umbrella
  family sits in the 1H-sword bucket, ANY class that can equip 1H
  swords can equip it. Realistically that's Warriors (native), but
  early-community-legend players used umbrellas on characters
  outside the meta as a novelty. The <em>Yellow</em> Umbrella in
  particular was talked about as a niche low-cost weapon in some
  v83 guides.</p>
  <p>Practically: it's a fine level-24 sword upgrade if you find
  one cheap in the market. Just know that when you show up to
  party quest wielding a parasol, people will notice.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Color variants:</strong> Yellow and Green Umbrellas
  live at adjacent WZ IDs and will get their own entries once
  they're touched by a chain or drop table worth citing. All
  three share the same base stats - the color is purely a
  cosmetic pick.
