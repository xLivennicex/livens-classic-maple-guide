# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>The very first proper wand in the Magician progression tree.
  You'll walk out of the <a href="/jobs/magician">Magician
  advancement</a> at level 8, hit up the Ellinia weapon shop, hand
  over 1.5k mesos, and this becomes your weapon for approximately
  the next ten levels.</p>
  <p>Wands attack slightly faster than staves at the cost of a
  little base magic attack, which is a fine tradeoff for early
  Magic Claw grinding. The Wooden Wand isn't scroll-worthy - by
  the time you'd have magic-attack scrolls, you're already looking
  at level 15+ upgrades - but it does its job for the price.</p>
