# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>The Blue Potion restores <strong>200 MP</strong> per use.
  It sits in the middle of the MP-restoration hierarchy: below
  Mana Elixir (300 MP), above the basic Mana Potion (100 MP).
  For most Magicians in the level 20-40 range, Blue Potions
  are the primary MP source during training.</p>

  <p><strong>Who needs this:</strong> primarily <a href="/jobs/magician">Magicians</a>
  (Fire Arrow at max level costs ~15 MP per cast, and you'll
  cast thousands per training session). Also useful for
  <a href="/jobs/warrior">Warriors</a> maintaining Rush /
  Booster uptime, <a href="/jobs/bowman">Bowmen</a> keeping
  Soul Arrow active, and <a href="/jobs/thief">Thieves</a>
  using Haste or Drain.</p>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong> Blue Potions drop from
  many mid-tier mobs (Wild Boar, Curse Eye, Zombie Mushroom).
  They're also sold at every town's potion NPC for ~110 mesos
  each. The 10x Blue Potion reward from
  <a href="/quests/talking-to-stan">Talking to Stan</a> is a
  significant early-game bulk grant - 2,000 MP of restoration
  for zero investment.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Buy in bulk.</strong> Potion NPCs sell Blue Potions
  in stacks of 100 for a bulk discount. Magicians training in
  the Ant Tunnel or Wild Boar Land at level 30-40 will burn
  through a full stack in a single training session, so buying
  three or four stacks between runs is normal Magician
  economy.
