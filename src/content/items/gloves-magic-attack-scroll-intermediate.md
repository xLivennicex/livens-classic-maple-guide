# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Intermediate tier upgrade scroll. When applied to any
  Gloves slot item, rolls a <strong>60% success chance</strong>;
  on success, adds <strong>+4 Magic Attack</strong>
  permanently to those gloves. On failure, the scroll is
  consumed and one of the gloves' upgrade slots is spent (with
  a small "boom" chance destroying the item outright).</p>

  <p><strong>Notice the +4, not +2.</strong> Unlike the
  <a href="/items/gloves-attack-scroll-intermediate">Weapon
  Attack variant</a> (which gives +2 WATT per success), the
  Magic Attack version gives <strong>+4 MATT</strong>. This is
  a systemic MapleStory pattern - Magic Attack scrolls
  historically give ~double the numeric bonus of Weapon
  Attack scrolls, because MATT scales differently in the
  damage formula.</p>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong> the primary source is
  <a href="/quests/cursed-doll-chain">Rowen's Cursed Doll Chain</a>
  tier 4 (150 Cursed Dolls) - drops one of these OR the Weapon
  Attack variant, randomly. Also sold in the Free Market when
  Warriors and Bowmen who wanted the WATT version dump the
  MATT rolls.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> <a href="/jobs/magician">Magicians</a>,
  specifically. Fire Arrow, Cold Beam, Thunder Bolt, Heal - every
  Magician damage skill scales off Magic Attack. Every +4 MATT
  from a scroll success is a meaningful uplift to per-hit
  damage across your entire kit.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Stacking math for Magicians:</strong> a Level 30
  glove has 5-7 slots. At 60% success rate you'll average 3-4
  successes, netting +12 to +16 Magic Attack. Combined with a
  well-scrolled wand or staff, that's often 15-20% more damage
  per cast at low-mid levels. Magicians should hoard these -
  Cursed Doll chain gives ONE tier-4 completion per character,
  but you can trade for more on the Free Market.
