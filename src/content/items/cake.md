editorial: |
  <p>Cake is a dual-stat food consumable: <strong>100 HP AND
  100 MP</strong> per use. That's uncommon in the food tier -
  most consumables restore one or the other. Cake's flexibility
  makes it a strong inventory pick for any class that uses
  both stat bars.</p>

  <p><strong>The math:</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>Red Potion: 100 HP, 5 mesos</li>
    <li>Blue Potion: 200 MP, 30 mesos</li>
    <li>Cake: 100 HP + 100 MP, 15 mesos</li>
  </ul>
  Per meso: Cake is dramatically better than buying Red +
  Blue separately (5+30=35 mesos for 100 HP + 200 MP, vs Cake
  at 15 mesos for 100 HP + 100 MP). If you're specifically
  running out of both HP and MP simultaneously, Cake wins
  on efficiency.</p>

  <p><strong>Where Cakes come from:</strong> primarily quest
  rewards. <a href="/quests/shumis-request">Shumi's Request</a>
  (Nella series step 2) awards 15 Cakes guaranteed. Occasional
  mid-level mob drops. Not commonly vendor-stocked.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Warriors during skill-heavy training:</strong>
      Power Strike + Slash Blast consume MP. When both bars
      drain simultaneously, Cake is one action instead of two.</li>
    <li><strong>Thieves using Lucky Seven / Double Stab:</strong>
      MP-intensive skills paired with squishy HP pool - Cake
      handles both crises at once.</li>
    <li><strong>Magicians managing Magic Guard:</strong> when
      Magic Guard converts damage to MP loss AND you're low
      on MP for your next cast, Cake resets both bars.</li>
  </ul></p>
