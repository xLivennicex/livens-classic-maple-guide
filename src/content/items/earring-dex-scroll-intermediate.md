# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Intermediate tier upgrade scroll. When applied to any
  Earring, rolls a <strong>60% success chance</strong>; on
  success, adds <strong>+2 DEX</strong> permanently to that
  earring. On failure, the scroll is consumed and one of the
  earring's upgrade slots is spent (with a small "boom" chance
  destroying the earring outright).</p>

  <p>Classic World's <a href="/quests/first-time-together">Kerning Party Quest</a>
  reward table gives one of these (or the STR, INT, or LUK
  variant, randomly) per completion. In v83 they were rare
  world drops; Classic World's PQ integration is the intended
  acquisition path.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> Bowmen (DEX is your
  damage stat), Thieves (DEX gates job advancement and the
  Assassin claw requirement), and Warriors who need to hit the
  next-tier weapon's DEX requirement. Basically everyone
  except pure Magicians finds a use.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Class overlap note:</strong> the DEX scroll is the
  most contested KPQ drop because 3 of the 4 base classes
  benefit meaningfully from it. If your party is heavy on
  ranged and rogue characters, expect to see DEX rolls get
  claimed first when the loot drops.
