editorial: |
  <p>Lemon is a food-tier MP consumable that restores
  <strong>~150 MP per use</strong>. Compare to Blue Potion
  (200 MP, sold everywhere) and Mana Elixir (50% MP with
  cooldown, rare). Lemon fits in the middle - stronger than
  most low-tier options, no cooldown, but not vendor-common.</p>

  <p><strong>Where Lemons come from:</strong> mostly quest
  rewards rather than vendor stock. <a href="/quests/don-hwangs-request">Don
  Hwang's Request</a> (Nella series) awards 15 Lemons. The
  <a href="/quests/henesys-community-board">Henesys Community
  Board</a> resident-check quests award 5 Lemons on some steps.
  Also drops from certain mid-level mobs.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> Magicians primarily.
  Magic Guard converts incoming damage to MP loss, so MP
  restore is Magician survivability. Lemons stack in your
  inventory alongside Blue Potions - many Magicians hotkey
  Lemons to a secondary slot for use when the Blue Potion
  hotkey depletes.</p>

  <p><strong>Warriors, Bowmen, Thieves</strong> use MP for
  skill activations (Power Strike, Double Shot, Lucky Seven,
  Double Stab). MP consumption is real but slower than a
  Magician's Magic Guard bleed. Lemons are worth keeping in
  small stacks (10-20) for skill-heavy training runs.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Food vs potion:</strong> Lemon is categorized as
  a "food" consumable, meaning it doesn't share cooldown with
  potion-category items (like Elixir). You CAN eat a Lemon
  and drink an Elixir back-to-back, stacking the restore
  effects. Useful in emergencies.
