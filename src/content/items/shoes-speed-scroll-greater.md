# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Greater tier upgrade scroll for the Shoes slot. When
  applied, rolls a <strong>10% success chance</strong>; on
  success, adds <strong>+3 Speed</strong> permanently. On
  failure (90% of the time!), the scroll is consumed AND
  the shoes are typically destroyed - Greater tier scrolls
  historically have a much higher boom rate than Intermediate.</p>

  <p><strong>The math is brutal.</strong> 10% success means
  you'll succeed once in ten attempts on average. That's ten
  scrolls consumed for one +3 Speed application. Multiply
  by shoe destruction rate and Greater scrolls have real
  meso cost per successful stat point applied.</p>

  <p><strong>When to use Greater instead of Intermediate:</strong>
  only when:
  <ul>
    <li>Your shoes have MANY remaining upgrade slots (10+)</li>
    <li>You already scrolled the safe slots with Intermediate</li>
    <li>You can afford to lose the shoes if they boom</li>
    <li>The +3 vs +2 differential matters for your build</li>
  </ul>
  For most players, Intermediate scrolls (60% success, +2)
  offer better expected value. Greater is a min-max tool.</p>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong> guaranteed drop from
  <a href="/quests/ronnies-house-building-chain">Ronnie's
  House-Building chain</a> final step (one per chain
  completion). Also drops rarely from higher-level mobs and
  sells on the Free Market at premium.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>The Greater trap:</strong> at 10% success rate,
  most players' first Greater scroll application is their
  last - the shoes boom and the scroll is gone. If you're
  new to scrolling, save the Greater for your SECOND pair
  of shoes after you've practiced with Intermediates.
