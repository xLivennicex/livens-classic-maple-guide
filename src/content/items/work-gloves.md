  <p>Work Gloves are the guaranteed reward from

  <p><strong>Why Critical Rate matters at L10:</strong>
  Critical Rate is a stat that most L10-30 gear doesn't offer.
  Even +1% means one extra crit per 100 hits - free damage
  compounding over every training session. Work Gloves are
  one of the earliest sources of this stat.</p>

  <p><strong>Why 5 upgrade slots matters:</strong> Gloves is
  a scroll-friendly slot. The endgame gloves-scrolling
  pipeline (unlocked at L36 via
  <a href="/quests/cursed-doll-chain">Cursed Doll chain</a>)
  awards <a href="/items/gloves-attack-scroll-intermediate">Gloves
  Attack Scroll (Intermediate)</a> at +2 Weapon Attack per
  successful scroll. Work Gloves at 5 slots can hold 5
  attempts. Even at 60% success rate, expect 2-3 successful
  scrolls = +4-6 WATT compounding on every attack.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> everyone. Work Gloves
  are marked "All" job requirement, so any base class or
  advanced class can wear them. The +1 CRT benefits all
  damage classes; the Weapon Defense is universally useful.</p>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Andre's Request (guaranteed):</strong> the
      Nella series step 3 hands one out. This is the standard
      path.</li>
    <li><strong>Vendor purchase:</strong> some Victoria Island
      general shops stock Work Gloves for 1,200 mesos.</li>
    <li><strong>Mob drops:</strong> Rare drops from various
      mid-level mobs.</li>
  </ul></p>
