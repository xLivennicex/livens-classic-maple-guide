# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Intermediate tier upgrade scroll. When applied to any
  Gloves slot item, rolls a <strong>60% success chance</strong>;
  on success, adds <strong>+2 Weapon Attack</strong>
  permanently to those gloves. On failure, the scroll is
  consumed and one of the gloves' upgrade slots is spent (with
  a small "boom" chance destroying the item outright).</p>

  <p><strong>Why gloves matter for Weapon Attack:</strong> gloves
  are one of the few slots where WATT scrolls exist AT ALL.
  Most equipment slots give raw defensive stats or ability-stat
  bonuses (STR/DEX/etc.); gloves are where you concentrate
  direct-damage upgrades. A single +2 WATT stack is often
  more damage than +2 STR.</p>

  <p><strong>How to acquire:</strong> the primary source is
  <a href="/quests/cursed-doll-chain">Rowen's Cursed Doll Chain</a>
  tier 4 (150 Cursed Dolls) - drops one of these OR the Magic
  Attack variant, randomly. Also occasionally sold in the Free
  Market by other players who rolled the wrong stat.</p>

  <p><strong>Who this is for:</strong> everyone except
  Magicians. Warriors, Bowmen, and Thieves all derive damage
  from Weapon Attack; every +2 stack is real DPS. Assassins
  specifically get double value because Lucky Seven's
  formula bakes in WATT alongside LUK.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Stacking math:</strong> Level 30 gloves have 5-7
  upgrade slots. At 60% success you'll average ~3-4 passes
  per glove, netting +6 to +8 WATT if you commit fully. Combined
  with a well-rolled weapon, this is the single largest
  glove-slot damage upgrade available before Zakum-tier
  content.
