# ==== Editorial ====
editorial: |
  <p>Squishy Shoes are the boss drop from
  <a href="/mobs/800003">King Slime</a>, the final-stage boss
  of the <a href="/party-quests/kerning-pq">Kerning Party
  Quest</a>. In v83 GMS these were called
  <strong>Slime Shoes</strong> and were arguably the single
  most-recognized KPQ reward in old-school Maple. Classic
  World renamed them but preserved the stats verbatim - this
  is the same iconic item under a new label, verified against
  the CoT 2 datamine (item ID 1072128).</p>

  <p><strong>Why they're the shoes equivalent of the
  <a href="/items/blue-sauna-robe">Sauna Robe</a>.</strong>
  Almost every other pair of shoes at L28-30 requires a
  specific job (Warrior Grieves, Mage Nitty, Bowman Boots,
  Thief Gidder Shoes) and gives DEF plus either Speed OR
  Jump. Squishy Shoes are <strong>Any Class</strong> and
  give <strong>+1 to every stat</strong> instead. That means:</p>

  <ul>
    <li><strong>Warriors:</strong> +1 STR is straight damage,
    and the +18 PDD is competitive with tier-appropriate
    warrior boots.</li>
    <li><strong>Magicians:</strong> +1 INT is meaningful at
    this level, and +7 MDD is the highest MDD you'll find on
    an L28 shoe.</li>
    <li><strong>Bowmen:</strong> +1 DEX is basically free
    damage and accuracy.</li>
    <li><strong>Thieves:</strong> +1 LUK is a big deal for
    Assassin and Bandit builds - see the
    <a href="/assassin-build">Assassin build guide</a> for
    why LUK-focused characters value every point they can
    stack.</li>
    <li><strong>Hybrid builds:</strong> the "wrong" stats
    aren't wasted for STR-Bandits, DEX-Fighters, or LUK-
    Magicians experimenting with off-meta approaches.</li>
  </ul>

  <p><strong>5 slots for scrolling.</strong> Not exceptional
  by L28-shoe standards (most are 5-7), but combined with the
  all-stat base makes them a great candidate for Shoes DEF
  scrolls, Shoes Speed scrolls, or Shoes Jump scrolls
  depending on your class's priority. See our
  <a href="/items/shoes-speed-scroll-intermediate">Shoes Speed
  Scroll: Intermediate</a> editorial for what to slot.</p>

  <p><strong>Comparison with L28 alternatives:</strong> at
  L28 the "best" alternative pair is the tier-appropriate
  job-specific boots (e.g. L28 Steel Grieves for Warriors).
  These give ~18 PDD + 1 Speed OR Jump, but no stat bonus.
  A well-scrolled Squishy Shoes beats them for most builds
  because the +4 raw stats compound with scrolling gains -
  though if you specifically need the +1 Speed or Jump for
  mobility, the specialized boots stay competitive.</p>

callout: |
  <strong>Naming note:</strong> Classic World rename is
  purely cosmetic - identical stats, identical drop source,
  identical progression role. The rename tracks a broader
  Classic World theme of softening the "cute" v83 item names
  ("Slime Shoes" was already cute; "Squishy Shoes" leans
  further in). If you see older guides referring to Slime
  Shoes for KPQ rewards, they mean this item.
