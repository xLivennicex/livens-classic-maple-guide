## What this page is

This is a curated signpost to **community-made Classic World content** - Reddit
threads, Google Sheets, YouTube videos, Discord compilations. We did not write
these resources and we don't verify every claim inside them. What we DO is read
each one carefully, note where the community has pushed back on it, and tell you
up front what the resource is good for.

The rest of this site - `/jobs`, `/assassin-build`, `/items`, `/quests`,
`/launch` - stays datamine-verified with sources you can check. **This page is
the one place where "community opinion" lives**, clearly labeled as such.

<div class="callout callout--heads-up">
<strong>Why bother with a resources page at all?</strong>
Because sometimes the best starting point for a decision is a build another
player has already drafted, even if it's imperfect. A disputed build you can
compare against the datamine beats staring at a blank SP window. The caveat
framing below is doing the heavy lifting: we show you both the resource AND
what the community has said about it.
</div>

<h2 id="how-we-pick-them">How we pick them</h2>

Every entry on this page meets three bars:

1. **Public and durable** - the resource is on a platform that doesn't vanish
   when a Discord server reshuffles. Reddit posts, Google Sheets, YouTube
   videos, GitHub repos all qualify. Private Discord screenshots do not.
2. **Attributable** - the author is named and the resource is datestamped. We
   want you to be able to tell if a build is from a 2026 Classic World tester
   versus a 2010 v83 fan-wiki.
3. **Honestly contextualized** - we read the top-upvoted comments, the author's
   edits, and any corrections. If the community pushed back on something, our
   framing captures that.

We **do not require** that we agree with every claim in a resource. We require
that you leave this page knowing what you're reading.

<h2 id="builds-and-sp-allocation">Builds &amp; SP allocation</h2>

### Complete Builds for all Classes - 1st & 2nd Job
*by u/xBucc on r/MSClassicWorld - Oct 2, 2026 - 540 upvotes*

**What it is:** An 11-image gallery of one-page SP build infographics covering
every 1st and 2nd job class, plus a
[Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dZS8OV7KEDHQxTDlNoRcG9Tc1BZKymiftm3PJ4neMck/edit?usp=sharing)
with per-skill rationale. Explicitly aimed at **free-to-play players with an
endgame / bossing mindset**. OP's stated goal: "these builds will not require
SP resets to be successful late game."

**Author's own caveat:** In the post's EDIT, OP acknowledged the **Archer
builds are wrong** ("The archer class is the least I've had the least
experience with so sorry for not doing my full research while making that
section"). The amended Archer builds live in the spreadsheet, NOT in the
original Reddit images.

**Where the community pushed back:**

- **Magician builds** (66-upvote reply): maxing Magic Claw from L12-L17 is
  argued to be a mana-cost trap during leveling. The counter-recommendation
  is to park Magic Claw at 4 points while investing in MP recovery.
- **Thief builds** (39-upvote reply): the Assassin build in the gallery
  reportedly does not max Dark Sight, which the replier called out as a core
  survival-skill omission.
- **Warrior builds** (28-upvote reply): all Warrior classes max both weapon
  mastery and booster, but skip Final Attack entirely. The replier argues
  Final Attack at 270% damage is too valuable to leave on the table,
  especially given F2P players scroll one weapon rather than two.
- **Page builds** (32-upvote reply): going hybrid gives up Final Attack, and
  Precise Strikes over HP recovery was called out as inappropriate for a
  game without a defined endgame yet.
- **F2P philosophy debate** (12-upvote reply): the resource prioritizes
  late-game bossing power. Counter-argument: F2P meso efficiency during
  leveling is more valuable because most players never make it to bossing
  levels, and Inkwell confirmed Cash Shop items will be 1-time tradable
  (changing the F2P calculus from the OSMS baseline).

**How to use it:** As a starting point to compare against the actual skill
tables on our individual job pages. The infographics give you one person's
opinionated end-state; the community discussion tells you where that opinion
is contested. Use both.

- **Original Reddit post:** [r/MSClassicWorld - Complete Builds for all Classes](https://www.reddit.com/r/MSClassicWorld/comments/1wvx41t/complete_builds_for_all_classes_1st_2nd_job/)
- **Google Sheet (with per-skill rationale):** [xBucc's build spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dZS8OV7KEDHQxTDlNoRcG9Tc1BZKymiftm3PJ4neMck/edit?usp=sharing)

<h2 id="submit-a-resource">Submit a resource</h2>

Found a community-made Classic World resource that belongs on this page?
Open an issue on
[the site's GitHub repo](https://github.com/xLivennicex/livens-classic-maple-guide/issues)
or drop a link somewhere Liven can find it. We'll read it, check the top
replies, and either add it with the honest-caveat framing or explain why we
passed. Resources that are pure link-farms, outdated for Classic World
specifically, or that pretend to be datamine-verified when they're not will
politely get skipped.
