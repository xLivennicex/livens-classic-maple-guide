## How to use this guide

Each entry below covers **one training map** with the same shape:

- **Level bracket** (when to consider it)
- **Location** — a link to the [map page](/maps) with the WZ ID
- **Mob roster** — links to individual [mob pages](/mobs)
- **Layout** — Eleveny's practical commentary on where to stand and how to rotate
- **Party fit** — how many players the spawn can support before efficiency drops
- **Key drops** — the reason to actually grind there, with links to [item pages](/items) where the WZ ID resolves

Every heading between L10 and L70 is a real map — nothing filler. Skip to the bracket that matches your current level.

> **A quick note on maple-island L1-10.** Beat up snails, do the tutorial quests, get yourself off Maple Island. Anything more detailed for that stretch would be its own separate guide. See the [Getting Started guide](/guides/getting-started) if you want a hand.

## Levels 10-15: off Maple Island

### Henesys Hunting Ground I — the front-door option

- **Map:** [Henesys Hunting Ground I](/maps/10001010) — one portal left from Henesys.
- **Mobs:** [Snail](/mobs/2), [Blue Snail](/mobs/3), [Red Snail](/mobs/5), [Slime](/mobs/7), [Orange Mushroom](/mobs/9), [Stump](/mobs/6), [Green Mushroom](/mobs/13). Lowest platforms are Maple-Island-tier; the map ramps up as you climb.
- **Layout:** Vertical tiered map. Sub-L10 players hug the bottom; Rogues and archers rotate to the upper Orange Mushroom platform; the highest-level players sit at the top with the Green Mushrooms.
- **Party fit:** Massive — the tier structure basically self-partitions players by level. Rule of thumb: **3 players per platform max** before spawn density starts hurting per-hour EXP. Channel-hop if it's crowded.
- **Why it wins:** Most accessible map in the game, and the only place where a fresh L10 and a L14 can train the same map without stepping on each other.

### Kerning City Construction Site — the Kerning alternative

- **Map:** [Kerning City Construction Site](/maps/10003010) — one map left of Kerning City.
- **Mobs:** [Stump](/mobs/6), [Snail](/mobs/2), [Orange Mushroom](/mobs/9), [Octopus](/mobs/12), [Green Mushroom](/mobs/13) at the top.
- **Layout:** Wide horizontal map, but all spawns cluster on the left side. Lower platforms hold Stumps + a few Mushrooms/Snails; the scaffolding stacks Octopus + Stumps + Orange Mushrooms across three floors; Green Mushrooms sit at the top.
- **Party fit:** Comparable to Hunting Ground I, and generally easier to spread out on. **Ranged classes should avoid the bottom-left** — the bricks and pipes block projectiles.
- **Why it wins:** Same EXP tier as Hunting Ground I with Kerning City proximity, meaning shorter potion runs if you're a Rogue or Bandit.

### Dungeon, Southern Forest I — "the Slime Tree"

- **Map:** [Dungeon, Southern Forest I](/maps/10002031) — three maps down and left of Ellinia.
- **Mobs:** [Slime](/mobs/7). Only Slime. Just Slime.
- **Layout:** Tight, cozy, three vertical tiers. Slimes bounce between platforms on their own, so even undercleared maps trickle mobs to the bottom for easy AoE gathering.
- **Party fit:** Best solo or with one other player. The map is genuinely small.
- **Key drop:** [Slime Bubble](/items/4000007) — quest item for several early chains, still sells to NPCs for ~10 mesos each. Not glamorous, but they drop frequently and stack.
- **Why it wins:** Uniform mob HP means uniform kill rhythm — no accuracy variance, no positioning weirdness. And the music is iconic.

### Callout: L10 weapons are the exception to Classic World's NPC-sell rebalance

> Nexon dramatically reduced equipment NPC sell prices across the board in Classic World, making gear-farming for meso far less profitable than in v83. **The L10 weapons in this bracket are one of the few exceptions** — they actually sell for MORE than they did originally. If you see one drop while training, hit F2 to loot and NPC it. This is genuinely one of the better early meso sources.

## Levels 15-20: the pig bracket

### The Pig Beach — hidden but worth it

- **Map:** [The Pig Beach](/maps/10000021) — hidden map behind the yellow flowers at the three-way road split on the way to Henesys.
- **Mobs:** [Ribbon Pig](/mobs/10), [Snail](/mobs/2), and a single [Iron Hog](/mobs/24) that respawns once per hour (the local-hero killer is basically a folk figure).
- **Layout:** Three training zones — flat section on the left, stair steps on the right, upper platforms. Ranged prefers left; melee prefers right (safer from Pig touch damage). There's a thorn at the bottom-right you can stand on to avoid touch damage entirely. Upper-platform players sweep left occasionally to clear Snails and keep spawn cycling.
- **Party fit:** 3-4 comfortable, one per zone.
- **Key drops:**
  - [Leather](/items/4000049) — crafting mat for the [Leatherworking discipline](/crafting)
  - [Stiff Feather](/items/4003002) — used in glove and cape recipes
  - [Pig's Head](/items/4000008) — quest ETC
  - [Iron Hog's Metal Hoof](/items/4000023) — from the Iron Hog specifically
- **Why it wins:** No single jackpot drop, but *constant* pickup value across three ETC items with real crafting demand. Great meso-per-hour floor.

### Caution Falling Down — party of six, perfectly

- **Map:** [Caution Falling Down](/maps/10003011) — hidden map from the top of Kerning City Construction Site.
- **Mobs:** [Octopus](/mobs/12), [Orange Mushroom](/mobs/9), [Blue Mushroom](/mobs/16) at the bottom.
- **Layout:** Multi-tier hallway with yellow barrier sniping spots for ranged. Melee can use the barriers too — the catch is an Orange Mushroom spawns directly on each barrier, so you're never fully safe. Bottom-floor Blue Mushrooms hit surprisingly hard; **highest-level party member takes the bottom** to soak.
- **Party fit:** Full party of 6 — 2 top, 2 middle, 1-2 bottom. Rare that a map splits this cleanly.
- **Why it wins:** One of the most-loved training maps in CoT 2 for a reason. Six-player parties feel efficient here in a way they rarely do at this level. Close to Kerning City for pot runs.

## Levels 20-25: Bubblings and KPQ

### Line 1 Area 1 — Bubbling paradise

- **Map:** [Line 1 &lt;Area 1&gt;](/maps/10003061) — inside the [Kerning City Subway](/maps/10003060).
- **Mobs:** [Bubbling](/mobs/14). Nothing else. Everywhere.
- **Layout:** Massively horizontal, dense spawn packing. Could probably fit 15+ players and still find mobs. Ranged and melee both work. **Rogues get a special treat** — platforms are just low enough that jump-throw stars can hit the platform above you, effectively doubling your AoE range.
- **Party fit:** Any size. Genuinely the best "somebody's already here, is that fine?" map on the island — the answer is basically always yes.
- **Why it wins:** Density and platform layout make it the mid-20s go-to for parties, and it's roomy enough that you never feel like you're stealing spawn.

### KPQ Bonus Stage — not the PQ, the bonus (and yes, Squishy Shoes are real)

- **Party quest:** [Kerning Party Quest](/party-quests/kerning-pq) — accessible at L21.
- **The trap:** In Classic World, Nexon **heavily reduced KPQ's EXP rewards**. Outside of the boss-drop chase and the Bonus Room, there isn't a strong reason to sit here farming runs. Two things make it worth showing up anyway.
- **Reason #1 — Squishy Shoes.** The iconic KPQ reward previously known as "Slime Shoes" was renamed **[Squishy Shoes](/items/1072128)** in Classic World but retained its identity: **L28 All-class, +1 STR / +1 DEX / +1 INT / +1 LUK, +18 PDD, 5 upgrade slots.** Confirmed dropping from [King Slime](/mobs/800003) (L40, 8,000 HP, boss in KPQ's Last Stage). This is the shoes equivalent of the [Blue Sauna Robe](/items/blue-sauna-robe) — one of the few universal-class stat-block pieces that any class can slot in, and it's the reason KPQ is still on this route.
- **Reason #2 — The Bonus Room.** After a successful KPQ clear, your party enters map [80000500](/maps/80000500) with a datamine-confirmed mob composition of **[Horny Mushroom](/mobs/19) x24 + [Green Mushroom](/mobs/13) x12**. Ideal setup: 4-player party, one floor per player.
- **Bonus Room drops worth chasing:**
  - **[Pan Lid](/items/1092002)** from Green Mushrooms — a Shield item that sells to players for real mesos.
  - **[Claw Attack Scroll: Intermediate](/items/2044701)** (60% success) can drop from Green Mushrooms. Free-market gold if you can flip it.
  - Horny Mushrooms drop several **L30 equips** that NPC for filler mesos while you're there.

## Levels 25-30: Ant Tunnel territory

### Damp Forest — surprise MVP

- **Map:** [Damp Forest](/maps/10005031) — in the Sleepywood dungeon area.
- **Mobs:** [Octopus](/mobs/12) x15, [Jr. Necki](/mobs/18) x11, [Slime](/mobs/7) x2. The Jr. Necki concentration is unusually high for a mid-20s map.
- **Layout:** Extremely tall. One platform sits far above the other two. **Always keep at least one player on the top platform** to keep spawn cycling, while 2-4 clear the lower levels.
- **Why it's newly good:** Historically, Jr. Neckis wrecked training spots because their accuracy demanded high DEX to hit. Classic World's **more forgiving accuracy requirements** flipped that on its head — now they're just another mob, and this map's Jr. Necki density makes it one of the best places on Victoria Island to farm **[Jr. Necki Skin](/items/4000018)** for quest chains.
- **PSA from Eleveny:** Don't try farming Jr. Necki Skins in the Kerning City sewers instead. It doesn't work.

### Ant Tunnel I (and Ant Tunnel IV) — the workhorse

- **Maps:** [Ant Tunnel I](/maps/10005060) and [Ant Tunnel IV](/maps/10005063) — deep in [Sleepywood](/maps/10005000).
- **Mobs:** [Horny Mushroom](/mobs/19), [Zombie Mushroom](/mobs/21).
- **Layout:** Comfortably supports a full party of 6, and can squeeze in another 3-4 before efficiency plummets. When Ant Tunnel I is packed, migrate to Ant Tunnel IV — they play almost identically.
- **Key drops:**
  - Multiple **L35 equips** from Zombie Mushrooms — respectable NPC-sell value while you train.
  - Buff potions — not useful at this level but they NPC for mesos in town.
- **Why it wins:** Right next to Sleepywood town = trivial repot runs. This is probably the highest-density workhorse map for L25-30.

### Land of Wild Boar — mid-20s hidden gem

- **Map:** [Land of Wild Boar](/maps/10004041) — hidden street behind a door, one map right of [Perion](/world/victoria-island).
- **Mobs:** [Wild Boar](/mobs/22) plenty, [Dark Axe Stump](/mobs/20) roaming, occasional [Jr. Boogie](/mobs/33) (5-10 minute respawn — the debuff annoyance is rare enough to tolerate).
- **Layout:** Three tiers. Bottom comfortably supports 2-3 players. Party of 3-5 is the sweet spot. Melee to the top, Rogues to the bottom (jump-throw hits the middle platform too).
- **Key drops (quantity over quality):**
  - [Leather](/items/4000049), [Animal Fur Bundle](/items/4000062), [Stiff Feather](/items/4003002) — steady crafting mat income
  - [Warrior Potion](/items/2002000) — situational free potions
  - **[Wooden Top](/items/2070009)** — one of the first throwing-star upgrades assassins want. Drops from Dark Axe Stumps.
  - **[Sap of Ancient Tree](/items/2012002)** — rare quest ETC that literally can't be farmed anywhere else. If someone's on that quest chain, they're paying you for these.

## Levels 30-40: the second-job power spike

Your second job hits at L30 and you're suddenly a real character. These maps let you exploit that.

### Burnt Land II — Fires & Throwing Stars

- **Map:** [The Burnt Land II](/maps/10004112).
- **Mobs:** [Fire Boar](/mobs/30), [Dark Axe Stump](/mobs/20). That's it.
- **Layout:** Two tiers. Comfortably shared between 2-3 players — 2 bottom, 1 top.
- **The real reasons to be here:**
  - **[Arwen's Glass Shoe](/items/4001000)** — rare quest ETC from Fire Boars. High free-market value to anyone doing the quest.
  - **[Diamond Ore](/items/4020007)** — drops from both mobs. One of the more valuable ores in Classic World, especially because a refined **[Diamond](/items/4020107)** is required for the **iconic [Sauna Robe quest](/quests/sleepywood-sauna-robe-chain)** — meaning demand is essentially universal. See the [Blue Sauna Robe](/items/blue-sauna-robe) / [Red Sauna Robe](/items/red-sauna-robe) item pages for why every class wants one.
  - **[Wolbi Throwing Stars](/items/2070001)** confirmed dropping from Fire Boars.
  - **[Wooden Top](/items/2070009)** from Dark Axe Stumps (continued from Land of Wild Boar).
- **Why it wins:** If you're an Assassin, this is arguably the best map on the island for you — you're farming your own throwing-star upgrades while grinding EXP. Consistent 30s training spot.

### Line 2 Area 1 — the Cleric map

- **Map:** [Line 2 &lt;Area 1&gt;](/maps/10003066) — deeper into the Kerning subway than Bubblings.
- **Mobs:** [Jr. Wraith](/mobs/32) everywhere, [Jr. Necki](/mobs/18) on the bottom floor.
- **Layout:** Massive — nearly 100 spawns. Upper platforms are wider than the Bubbling map, better suited for ranged. **Fire/Poison Wizards and Clerics live here** because Jr. Wraiths are weak to both fire and holy damage. Fire Arrow and Holy Arrow dominate. Melee classes can lure the bottom floor to one side and AoE them down old-school.
- **Party fit:** Almost any size. Great choice for the awkward "we have 5 people but not 6" party.
- **Key drops:**
  - Several **L35 equips** with decent NPC value
  - **[Mokbi Throwing Stars](/items/2070002)** — the real prize, next star tier up for Assassins.

### The Cave of Evil Eye I — vertical AoE heaven

- **Map:** [The Cave of Evil Eye I](/maps/10005071) — deeper Ant Tunnel, past [Ant Tunnel Park](/maps/10005070).
- **Mobs:** Mostly [Evil Eye](/mobs/23), plus two [Jr. Boogie](/mobs/33) with 5-minute respawn.
- **Layout:** Extremely vertical. Large gaps between platforms mean you can drop down fast, and mobs naturally pull to the bottom. **Best for shorter-range AoE skills** — Ice/Lightning Wizards and Warriors specifically. Repot at Ant Tunnel Park without ever going back to town.
- **Party fit:** 2-4 works well.
- **Note:** Caves II-IV ([III](/maps/10005073), [IV](/maps/10005074)) are comparable — rotate if I is crowded.

## Levels 40-50: party play

### Dangerous Valley — the elementalist smile

- **Map:** [Dangerous Valley](/maps/10004101) — hidden street southeast of Perion, behind a door.
- **Mobs:** [Fire Boar](/mobs/30) alongside [Copper Drake](/mobs/40). Both weak to ice.
- **Layout:** Spread-out platforms. Full party of 6 fits comfortably.
- **Why it wins:** **Ice/Lightning Wizards** eat this map alive — every mob is weak to their primary element. Layout also happens to be one of the most party-friendly on the island because platforms are placed for genuine spread rather than "everybody stack top".

### The Valley of Death — the drops map

- **Map:** [The Valley of Death](/maps/10006031) — in Classic World's exclusive [Forgotten Hollow](/hollow) zone.
- **Mobs:** [Rafflesia](/mobs/59) x10 on lower two floors, [Zombie Mushroom](/mobs/21) x8 and [Zombie Lupin](/mobs/38) x8 on upper floors.
- **Layout:** Obvious party map — four floors, one role per floor.
- **Why every class wants to be here:**
  - Rafflesia has an **excellent EXP-to-HP ratio**, is slow, and is wide → comfy for ranged and melee.
  - Rafflesia has a chance to drop **[Earring LUK Scroll: Intermediate](/items/earring-luk-scroll-intermediate)** (60% success, +2 LUK) — a top-priority scroll for Magicians and Thieves. See the [Assassin build guide](/assassin-build) for why LUK-focused characters value earring scrolls above almost anything else.
  - Zombie Mushrooms drop **[Cursed Doll](/items/4000033)** — needed for the Ellinia quest chain that rewards a L40 hat. Old game needed ~2,000 dolls; Classic World reduced this to a few hundred.
  - Zombie Lupins drop **[Black Napoleon](/items/1102008)** — currently one of the best capes for Magicians and Thieves at **+3 LUK average**.
  - **Fire/Poison Wizards and Clerics** have an especially good time — Rafflesia weakness on bottom, Zombie mob weakness up top.

### Drake's Meal Table — the Drake grinder

- **Map:** [Drake's Meal Table](/maps/10005090) — right past the Evil Eye caves in Ant Tunnel.
- **Mobs:** [Drake](/mobs/45) as the main event, few [Jr. Boogie](/mobs/33) and [Horny Mushroom](/mobs/19) tucked in the top-right corner.
- **Layout:** Four flat tiers, stacked. 3-4 players split comfortably. Clear the upper corners occasionally to keep spawn cycling.
- **Why warriors love it:** Drakes have **large hitboxes** and move fast, which is basically the ideal AoE-grouping monster for Warrior training. Ancient AoE-luring strategies apply.
- **Key drops:**
  - **[Dragon Skin](/items/4000050)** — high demand for glove and shoe crafting recipes. Always a buyer.
  - **[Drake's Blood](/items/2012000)** — either chug for a short but powerful Weapon Attack buff, OR sell to players working the quest chain that eventually rewards a random glove scroll.

### Line 3 (Wraiths) — the Red Whip stakeout

- **Map:** Deep [Kerning Subway](/maps/10003060), a couple maps past Line 2's Jr. Wraiths.
- **Mobs:** [Wraith](/mobs/43) in force.
- **Layout:** Traditionally the quintessential Cleric farming spot. Classic World nerfed heal-AoE, so Clerics now spam Holy Arrow instead of trivializing the map.
- **Key drops:**
  - **[Red Whip](/items/1302011)** — the reason literally anyone comes here. **+15 Speed** on a common-tier weapon, still the go-to movement-speed item for any class tired of walking at Victoria Island base speed.
  - **[Medicine With Weird Vibes](/items/4000039)** — the Wraith signature ETC, used in **five different quests** across Victoria Island. Steady demand.
- Excellent EXP-to-HP ratio makes this a strong choice even if you're just here for pure grinding.

## Levels 50-60: endgame Victoria

### The End of Fleeting Light — mirror-map party heaven

- **Map:** [The End of Fleeting Light](/maps/10006070) — in [Forgotten Hollow](/hollow).
- **Mobs:** [Aqumander](/mobs/57) x9 (weak to lightning), [Duskmander](/mobs/60) x7 (weak to ice), [Rafflesia](/mobs/59) x11 on upper platforms.
- **Layout:** Two mirrored halves — Rafflesia occupy the upper four platforms, salamanders roam the lower levels. **Not really an elemental grinding map** despite the weaknesses — the two salamander types are so heavily mixed that constant element swapping isn't practical. This is a "layout + density" map, not an elemental min-max map.
- **Party fit:** Perfect full party of 6, one per platform.
- **Why it wins:** One of the cleanest 1-role-per-player maps in the game.

### The Entrance of Golem's Temple — Stone Golem heaven

- **Map:** [The Entrance of Golem's Temple](/maps/10001102) — hidden behind a warning sign on the map between the Henesys region and Sleepywood entrance.
- **Mobs:** [Stone Golem](/mobs/48), [Blue Mushroom](/mobs/16), [Fairy](/mobs/25) 1-4.
- **Layout:** Stone Golems have **massive hitboxes** — from the upper platforms you'll often hit mobs above and below you at the same time with normal attacks. Same effect at the bottom, where ranged classes can hit the second-lowest platform while standing safely on the floor. Blue Mushrooms and Fairies are bonus EXP that you'll delete without thinking.
- **THE big drop:**
  - **[Tobi Throwing Stars](/items/2070004)** — one of the best throwing stars in the game. Incredible upgrade for Assassins, and a huge payday for anyone else who sees it drop.
- **Why it wins:** Even ignoring Tobi, Stone Golems are one of the most viscerally satisfying training targets in the game — the big-hitbox AoE overlap feels great on every class.

### Another Sanctuary — capstone Victoria map

- **Map:** [Another Sanctuary](/maps/10006162) — deep in [Forgotten Hollow](/hollow).
- **Mobs:** [Rotten Mushroom](/mobs/62) x10 (upper), [Sporewood](/mobs/63) x7 (lower — stronger), [Golden Stirge](/mobs/56) x3 mixed in.
- **Layout:** Cleanly divided — Rotten Mushrooms upstairs, stronger Sporewoods downstairs.
- **Why it might be your last efficient Victoria stop:**
  - Both main mobs are weak to holy → **Clerics are extremely valuable party members here**.
  - Both mobs appear capable of dropping L50-60 equipment (exact drop tables still being nailed down).
  - Eleveny's take: "This may be one of the last truly efficient grinding spots available on Victoria Island."

## Levels 60-70: loot over EXP

> **The wall.** Once you cross L60, EXP gains fall off hard. Monster EXP-to-HP ratios drop dramatically. If your goal is pure EXP, **you're actually better off staying at L50-tier maps** (End of Fleeting Light, Golem's Temple, Another Sanctuary). The maps below are worth it for **drops**, not raw EXP-per-hour. See our [Party EXP guide](/guides/party-exp) for how party composition can mitigate this.

### Dangerous Valley II — chasing Steely

- **Map:** [Dangerous Valley II](/maps/10004102) — one map beyond [Dangerous Valley](/maps/10004101) near Perion.
- **Mobs:** The strongest Drakes available on Victoria Island. Among the first mobs many players encounter that use **magic attacks** — expect to eat hits harder than you're used to.
- **The prize:**
  - **[Steely Throwing Knives](/items/2070005)** — the famous chase drop. The tier just above Tobi. Assassin bank breaker.

### Sanctuary Entrance IV — the taro pit

- **Map:** [Sanctuary Entrance IV](/maps/10005104) — deep Ant Tunnel past Cursed Sanctuary.
- **Mobs:** [Tauromacis](/mobs/52) x2, [Taurospear](/mobs/53) x5, [Cold Eye](/mobs/23) x5.
- **The reality check:** EXP-to-HP is *still* bad here, and **unavoidable Lightning Strike attacks** hurt your efficiency further. You are here for the loot lottery, not the grind.
- **Why show up anyway:** These mobs *might* drop some of the best gear currently available at this tier. Depending on how quickly Ossyria arrives at launch, they may be worth farming for a limited window before the map metagame shifts entirely.

## What Eleveny didn't cover (and why we didn't either)

- **[Kerning Party Quest](/party-quests/kerning-pq) grinding for EXP** — nerfed, skip except for bonus stage and shoes.
- **The exact "best of the best" claim for every level.** Eleveny's video and this guide highlight *maps he considers strongest* per bracket, but Classic World is new and community rankings will shift. Cross-reference the [maps index](/maps) or drop by the [hidden maps guide](/maps/hidden) if you want to explore alternatives.
- **Elemental min-maxing.** Several maps (Line 2 Area 1, Dangerous Valley, End of Fleeting Light) list elemental weaknesses, but this guide doesn't go deep on element math. That's a separate write-up for the future.
