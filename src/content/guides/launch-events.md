<h2 id="journey-begins">The Journey Begins - daily check-in</h2>

**Who:** characters Lv. 10 and above. **Where:** Maple Administrator.

1. Accept **'[Event] The Journey Begins'** from Maple Administrator.
2. Monsters Lv. 10 and above can drop [Maple Leaf](/items/4031134).
3. Trade **30 Maple Leaves** to Maple Administrator to claim that day's reward.

**Once per day, per account.** Rewards follow how many check-ins you have
completed, not the calendar - missing days doesn't reset you. Claim Day 1
on Monday, come back Wednesday, and you get Day 2. Each day's reward can
be claimed once per account, so alts don't double-dip.

expire 7 days after you receive them - claim the store permits and
megaphones when you'll actually use them.

<h2 id="marks-we-leave-behind">The Marks We Leave Behind - the Mark of the Beta</h2>

**Who:** characters Lv. 25 and above. **Where:** Jay, in Henesys.

Accept **'[Event] The Marks We Leave Behind'** from Jay, then follow the
quest's clues around Victoria Island to a hidden treasure: the
**[Mark of the Beta](/items/1002994)**. Runs until November 18, 11:59 PM UTC.

<div class="callout callout--framing">
<strong>Datamine, not release notes:</strong> our client datamine lists
the Mark of the Beta as a hat with +1 to all stats, +15 DEF, and 7
upgrade slots. Nexon's notes don't give its stats, and they don't say
whether it is per character or per account - we'll update after
in-game verification.
</div>

<h2 id="classic-beginners">To Classic Beginners - rings for test players</h2>

If you completed either of these quests during Closed Online Test #1
and/or #2:

- '[Event] A Strange and Familiar Wood'
- '[Event] Beyond the Unknown'

you receive the [Classic Beginnings Label Ring](/items/1112108) and the
[Classic Beginnings Chat Ring](/items/1112219) by **Mailbox**. Completing
the event in one test gets one of each ring; completing it in both
tests gets two of each.

<h2 id="halloween-candies">Halloween Candies - and the Pumpkin Basket</h2>

**Who:** characters Lv. 10 and above. **When:** Oct 28 - Nov 18.
**Where:** Malady, in Henesys and Kerning City.

1. Accept **'[Event] Halloween Candies'** from Malady.
2. Monsters Lv. 10 and above can drop [Halloween Candies](/items/4031129).
3. Bring **100 Halloween Candies** back to Malady.

There's also a **hidden mission**: hand the candies out to children
around Victoria Island. Its reward is the **[Pumpkin Basket](/items/1302019)**,
tradable within world and permanent.

| Pumpkin Basket | Nexon's release notes | Our pre-launch datamine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type | One-handed Sword | 1H Sword |
| Attack Speed | Fast | - |
| Weapon Attack | **+27** | +24 |
| Speed | **+3** | +8 |
| All stats | not listed | +1 STR / DEX / INT / LUK |
| Enhancements | 7 | 7 upgrade slots |

The two sources disagree on Weapon Attack and Speed. The datamine comes
from a pre-launch client, so treat Nexon's numbers as current until
someone checks one in-game.

<h2 id="slime-attack">Slime Attack</h2>

**Who:** characters Lv. 10 and above. **When:** Oct 28 - Nov 18.

Orange Slimes appear in **Henesys, Ellinia, Kerning City, and Perion**
and can drop:

| Drop | Conditions | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Halloween Candies](/items/4031129) | Untradable, expires Nov 18, 11:59 PM UTC | Counts toward Malady's quest |
| [Red Malady's Candy](/items/2020020) | Tradable, permanent | Recovers 600 HP |
| [Blue Malady's Candy](/items/2020021) | Tradable, permanent | Recovers 450 MP |
| [Green Malady's Candy](/items/2020022) | Tradable, permanent | Restores 300 HP and 300 MP |

The two events stack: slimes in town feed the candy count for Malady.

<h2 id="gm-events">GM events</h2>

Shared rules for all three:

- When the GM opens the **Event Door**, talk to **Paul at Lith Harbor** to enter.
- You **can't** enter if you own a [Scroll of Secrets](/items/4031065) or joined a GM event in the past 24 hours.
- There are several rounds and rooms fill fast - if you miss one, try the next round.
- Everyone who takes part gets an [Event Trophy](/items/4001009).

| Event | UTC | Pacific | Format | Time limit | Scroll of Secrets to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jump-quest challenge | Oct 22, 12:00 - 2:00 AM | Oct 21, 5:00 - 7:00 PM | Jump quest | 15 min | everyone who completes it |
| Coconut Harvest | Nov 6, 12:00 - 2:00 AM | Nov 5, 4:00 - 6:00 PM | Two teams knock coconuts from trees | 5 min | the winning team |
| Up And Up | Dec 19, 12:00 - 2:00 AM | Dec 18, 4:00 - 6:00 PM | Three stages of ladders and portals | 6 min | everyone who reaches the last stage |

Special rules:

- **Jump quest and Up And Up:** default movement and jump only - Haste and Teleport are disabled and consumables have no effect. In Up And Up, a wrong portal can send you back to the start.
- **Coconut Harvest:** melee only - ranged attacks and skills are disabled. Harry sells melee weapons inside the event map, so Bowmen and Magicians can still play.

<h2 id="magic-box">Scroll of Secrets and the Magic Box</h2>

1. Take your Scroll of Secrets to **Chun Ji at Kerning City Construction Site** and complete his quest for a [Magic Box](/items/4031067).
2. Give the Magic Box to **Mr. Pickall** in Kerning City for **one random item** from the list below.

Remember the catch from the GM-event rules: holding an unspent Scroll of
Secrets locks you out of the next GM event. Exchange it before then.
