You hit Level 70, finished 2nd job, and now Victoria Island feels tired.
Welcome to the FA endgame — a 30-level stretch where the content ceiling
is real but the preparation window is more valuable than it looks.
This guide answers four questions:

1. **What are you supposed to DO with Lv 70-100?** (Short answer: finish
   2nd-job skills, scroll gear, bank mesos, hold your 3rd-job SP.)
2. **What SP do you need to hold back for 3rd job?** (Depends on branch.
   Table below.)
3. **What gear should you have scrolled by the time Grand Launch opens?**
   (Earrings, weapon, overall, gloves. Prioritized list below.)
4. **What's unlocking Oct 21 and what should you expect from your 3rd job?**
   (Per-class preview below with explicit "confirmed vs guessing" flags.)

<h2 id="the-l70-reality">The L70 reality: content gets thin</h2>

Here's the honest ceiling, pulled straight from the FA roster:

- **Max training maps** stop giving great EXP past ~Lv 70. The deeper
  Forgotten Hollow maps (Dreaming Trees, Deep Hollow) are the top FA
  options and plateau around Lv 80.
- **Bosses accessible at Lv 70+**: just [Mushmom](/bosses/mushmom) (L60)
  and [Zombie Mushmom](/bosses/zombie-mushmom) (L65). Both become EXP
  afterthoughts past Lv 80 — their 1,200-1,500 EXP is a rounding error.
- **Zakum, Papulatus, Pianus** — all **held for Grand Launch** per
  Nexon's Oct 3 release notes.
- **Orbis / Leafre / El Nath Lion King's Castle** — not in FA. The maps
  exist in the datamine but the portals/warps aren't live.

**Practical consequence:** your Lv 70-100 run is slower than Lv 30-70
was. Expect ~20-30 hours of grinding per 10 levels past Lv 70 depending
on class and party composition. Full party XP (+170% in a 6-player
party per the FA client values) is the single biggest accelerator.

<h2 id="what-to-do-with-30-levels">What to actually do with 30 levels</h2>

Prioritize in this order:

### 1. Finish your 2nd-job SP

If you reached Lv 70 with any unspent 2nd-job SP, finish those skill
maxes first. 2nd job caps your SP pool anyway — holding SP for 3rd job
only matters AFTER your 2nd-job core skills are maxed.

### 2. Scroll your weapon + glove

The biggest damage-per-meso lever. 60% ATT scrolls on your Lv 50-70
weapon get you +8-10 ATT on average (burning ~2 scrolls per net +2).
See the [gearing guide](/gearing-30-plus#scrolling-philosophy) for the
60% ATT math. **This gear carries into 3rd job.** You don't replace
your Lv 60 weapon until Lv 90+ equipment unlocks post-launch — scroll
your Lv 60 well and it's your Grand Launch starter.

### 3. Scroll your earrings

[Classic World added a crit-damage line to earring items](/gearing-30-plus#classic-world-ground-rules).
This makes earrings a damage upgrade, not just a stat stick. Every
build should have scrolled earrings by Grand Launch. Burn 60% stat
scrolls (DEX/LUK/INT/STR appropriate to your class).

### 4. Bank mesos

Grand Launch opens Zakum, Horntail, and the full continent map. The
scrolls, crafting mats, and summoning sacks you'll need will cost
mesos. Target bank balance: **5-10 million mesos** by Oct 21.

### 5. Grind out your character-defining content

- Finished [Henesys Community Board quests](/quests/henesys-community-board)? They give AP + meso rewards.
- [Alex's Request](/quests/alexs-request) chain complete?
- [Cursed Doll](/quests/cursed-doll-chain) chain for the LUK hat?
- [Sauna Robe](/quests/sleepywood-sauna-robe-chain) for the +3 WATT top?

The reward-vs-time ratio drops as you level, but if you missed any of
these during 30-70, Lv 70+ is the catch-up window.

<h2 id="sp-holdback-rules">SP holdback rules per 3rd-job branch</h2>

**At Lv 70 you unlock 3rd-job advancement** per the FA datamine's
level-gating tables. But in Founder's Access, **3rd job advancement
is held for Grand Launch** (Oct 21). Hold your 3rd-job SP accordingly.

### How much SP will you have "banked" at Lv 100?

Every level past 70 gives 3 SP. Levels 70-100 = 30 levels = **90 SP
banked** by the time Grand Launch opens if you cap at Lv 100 before
Oct 21. That's enough to immediately max 2-3 core 3rd-job skills on
Day 1 of Grand Launch.

### Per-branch SP holdback priorities

| Branch | Max first (Day 1 of 3rd job) | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero (Fighter)** | Rush, Combo Attack | Rush is mobility + stun; Combo stacks for the Brandish bomb |
| **Paladin (Page)** | Charge Blow, Threaten | Charge adds elemental damage to your 2nd-job attack chain |
| **Dark Knight (Spearman)** | Beholder, Dragon Buster | Beholder gives you a pet HP buff; DB is your main attack |
| **F/P Mage** | Teleport Mastery, Explosion | Explosion is the F/P training spell; TM is QoL |
| **I/L Mage** | Teleport Mastery, Ice Strike | Ice Strike is your mobbing spell; same TM logic |
| **Priest (Cleric)** | Holy Symbol, Mystic Door | Holy Symbol is +50% party EXP; Door is the trade/teleport QoL |
| **Ranger (Hunter)** | Mortal Blow, Puppet | Mortal Blow procs for mob clear; Puppet is boss tanking |
| **Sniper (Crossbowman)** | Mortal Blow, Puppet | Same priorities as Ranger - crossbow is slower but higher-crit |
| **Night Lord (Assassin)** | Shadow Partner, Avenger | Shadow Partner is the Assassin DPS multiplier - max it FIRST |
| **Chief Bandit (Bandit)** | Meso Guard, Band of Thieves | BoT is your mob clearer; Meso Guard preserves your bank |

<div class="callout">
<strong>Holy Symbol = +50% party EXP buff.</strong> If a Priest in your
Grand Launch party maxes Holy Symbol first, every mob you kill as a
party gives +50% EXP for 2 minutes per cast. The single most valuable
party support skill in the game. If you ARE the Priest, this is your
Day 1 contribution.
</div>

<h2 id="gear-finishing-list">Gear finishing list before Grand Launch</h2>

Target state by Oct 21:

| Slot | Target | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Weapon** | Lv 50-70 scrolled with 60% ATT | S-tier - carries into 3rd job |
| **Gloves** | Lv 40+ scrolled with 60% ATT | S-tier - carries into 3rd job |
| **Earrings** | Lv 50+ scrolled with 60% stat | A-tier - new CW crit-damage line |
| **Overall** | Lv 50-60 scrolled with 60% stat (STR/DEX/LUK/INT) | A-tier - cheap scrolls, big stat delta |
| **Hat** | Lv 45+ scrolled with 60% stat | B-tier - replaceable at L70+ |
| **Shoes** | Lv 40+ with speed scrolls | B-tier - QoL not DPS |
| **Cape** | [Green/Blue Icarus Cape](/quests/10321) L46 or Gaia L65 | B-tier - the FA ceiling is Gaia |

### Items NOT worth your scrolls

- **Capes above the Gaia Lv 65 tier** — don't exist in FA. Hard-scroll
  your Gaia and move on. L70+ capes land with Orbis at Grand Launch.
- **Zakum Helmet-tier INT scrolls** — the altar isn't live. You can't
  tag Zakum in FA.
- **Chaos Scroll gambles** — datamined but community-confirmed drop
  sources haven't surfaced. Hold until Day-14+ intel.

<h2 id="meso-targets">Meso targets per playstyle</h2>

Rough bank-balance targets by Oct 21:

- **Casual (1-2 hr/day)**: 2M mesos. Enough for Zakum entry fees + a
  stack of 60% scrolls.
- **Regular (3-5 hr/day)**: 5-10M mesos. Enough for a full re-scroll
  of weapon and gloves if your first attempt bricks.
- **Grinder (5+ hr/day)**: 15M+ mesos. Enough to white-scroll gamble
  the moment WS acquisition becomes clear post-launch.

Primary meso sources at Lv 70+:

1. **[Mushmom](/bosses/mushmom) and [Zombie Mushmom](/bosses/zombie-mushmom)** —
   boss drops include ETC-sellables and consumable NPC-trash. ~50-200K
   per clear depending on luck.
2. **Deep Forgotten Hollow** — the dead-end maps have great mob density
   with mid-tier NPC-sellable gear drops.
3. **Quest completion windfalls** — Henesys Community Board, Sauna Robe,
   Cursed Doll chains if you haven't finished them.

See the [full meso-farming guide](/meso-farming-30-plus) for per-mob
yields.

<h2 id="third-job-at-a-glance">3rd job at a glance</h2>

What Nexon has confirmed and what we're guessing:

**Confirmed (per Nexon's Oct 3 release notes and the FA datamine):**

- 3rd job unlocks at **Lv 70**.
- 3rd job advancement **starts available Oct 21, 2026** (Grand Launch).
- The 10 3rd-job branches follow traditional MapleStory lineage
  (Fighter → Hero, Page → Paladin, etc.).

**NOT confirmed (don't trust specific numbers until Oct 21):**

- Exact skill tunings — several 3rd-job skills aren't yet exposed in
  the FA client's skill definitions.
- Whether Nexon re-tunes 3rd-job skills (as they did for 1st-job, where
  every class now shares a unified Lv 10 cap).
- The exact Lv 70 advancement quest and its NPC location.
- Whether Zakum opens Day 1 of Grand Launch or lags behind.

The sections below give per-class previews. Each branch's section flags
**[CONFIRMED]** vs **[HELD FOR GRAND LAUNCH]** so you can calibrate how
much to trust what you read.

<h2 id="warrior-third-jobs">Warrior 3rd jobs</h2>

### Hero (from Fighter)

**Role:** sustained melee DPS with Combo Attack stacking.

- **[CONFIRMED]** Hero unlocks from Fighter at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit present in FA datamine: Rush (mobility +
  stun), Combo Attack (stack 1-5 orbs for damage multiplier), Advanced
  Combat (ATK boost), Brandish (the Combo bomb).
- **[HELD]** Exact Combo Attack per-orb damage multiplier.
- **Day 1 priority:** Rush → Combo Attack. Combo transforms your
  rotation; the sooner you get it, the sooner your DPS explodes.
- **Prep note:** Hero scales off your Lv 50-70 2H sword or axe. Scroll
  that weapon with 60% ATT during FA — it carries 10-20 levels into
  Hero before you replace.

### Paladin (from Page)

**Role:** off-tank with elemental damage switching.

- **[CONFIRMED]** Paladin unlocks from Page at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Charge Blow (elemental damage), Threaten
  (debuff), Advanced Charge, Elemental Charges (Fire/Ice/Lightning).
- **[HELD]** Exact Charge Blow elemental multiplier under CW's 1.25×
  rules (vs the traditional 1.5× curve). This is a top open question.
- **Day 1 priority:** Charge Blow + a Fire or Ice Charge. The pairing
  gives you element-switch damage against boss weak-points.
- **Prep note:** Paladin's HP pool question (no HP wash) is the top
  endgame debate — see the [Page guide](/page-build) for details.

### Dark Knight (from Spearman)

**Role:** Hyper Body party support with Dragon Buster sustain.

- **[CONFIRMED]** Dark Knight unlocks from Spearman at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Beholder (pet HP buff), Dragon Buster
  (main attack), Crusher (combo attack), Hyper Body carry-over from
  2nd job (party HP +60%).
- **[HELD]** Beholder healing-tick values.
- **Day 1 priority:** Beholder → Dragon Buster. Beholder is a +1
  party-slot of raw HP sustain; it's the single best DK contribution.
- **Prep note:** Hyper Body remains top-tier support in CW because
  HP wash removal makes +60% HP a bigger relative buff than it was
  in traditional MapleStory. DK stays in every party.

<h2 id="magician-third-jobs">Magician 3rd jobs</h2>

### F/P Mage

**Role:** burst elemental AoE with Explosion.

- **[CONFIRMED]** F/P Mage unlocks from Wizard (F/P) at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Explosion (AoE spell), Poison Breath,
  Teleport Mastery (QoL), Partial Resistance.
- **[HELD]** Explosion mana cost and damage per hit.
- **Day 1 priority:** Teleport Mastery → Explosion. TM makes you
  mobile; Explosion makes you clear.
- **Prep note:** CW's 1.25× elemental multiplier (not 1.5×) means
  F/P's fire-weak-mob advantage is softer than traditional. Scroll
  for raw MATK over "match element at any cost."

### I/L Mage

**Role:** Ice Strike freeze-lock mobbing.

- **[CONFIRMED]** I/L Mage unlocks from Wizard (I/L) at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Ice Strike (AoE freeze), Thunder Spear,
  Teleport Mastery, Element Amplification.
- **[HELD]** Ice Strike freeze duration under CW's debuff rules.
- **Day 1 priority:** Teleport Mastery → Ice Strike. Freeze-lock makes
  high-HP mob camps trivial.
- **Prep note:** Element Amp is the mage damage multiplier — don't
  skip it for pure cost reasons.

### Priest (from Cleric)

**Role:** party support with Holy Symbol EXP buff.

- **[CONFIRMED]** Priest unlocks from Cleric at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Holy Symbol (+party EXP), Shining Ray
  (holy AoE), Mystic Door (party teleport), Dispel.
- **[HELD]** Holy Symbol exact % buff (traditional was +50%, CW
  value awaiting live confirmation).
- **Day 1 priority:** Holy Symbol → Shining Ray. HS makes you the
  most-invited party member in the game.
- **Prep note:** CW's HP wash removal is the Cleric community's top
  anxiety — Priest/Bishop endgame survival is an open question. See
  [the Cleric guide's HP wash section](/cleric-build#the-hp-wash-removal-reality).

<h2 id="bowman-third-jobs">Bowman 3rd jobs</h2>

### Ranger (from Hunter)

**Role:** AoE bow damage with Strafe + Arrow Rain.

- **[CONFIRMED]** Ranger unlocks from Hunter at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Strafe (4-hit single-target), Arrow Rain
  (AoE), Mortal Blow (chance to one-shot), Puppet, Thrust.
- **[HELD]** Strafe damage per hit and Arrow Rain radius.
- **Day 1 priority:** Mortal Blow → Puppet. MB procs on mobs below
  25% HP for instant kill; Puppet distracts bosses.
- **Prep note:** Ranger scales off scrolled bow + DEX. Scroll your
  Lv 50-70 bow with 60% ATT now.

### Sniper (from Crossbowman)

**Role:** burst crossbow damage with Strafe + Blizzard.

- **[CONFIRMED]** Sniper unlocks from Crossbowman at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Strafe (4-hit single-target), Blizzard
  (AoE), Mortal Blow, Puppet, Thrust.
- **[HELD]** Strafe critical rate interaction with CW's new earring
  crit-damage stat line.
- **Day 1 priority:** Mortal Blow → Puppet. Same as Ranger.
- **Prep note:** Sniper's higher crit rate means CW's earring crit-
  damage line is MORE valuable for you than for Ranger. Scroll
  crit-earrings aggressively.

<h2 id="thief-third-jobs">Thief 3rd jobs</h2>

### Night Lord (from Assassin)

**Role:** burst throwing-star DPS with Shadow Partner.

- **[CONFIRMED]** Night Lord unlocks from Assassin at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Shadow Partner (clone your attacks for
  +X% damage), Avenger (AoE), Alchemist (potion boost), Taunt.
- **[HELD]** Shadow Partner exact damage multiplier (traditional was
  70% but CW value awaiting live confirmation).
- **Day 1 priority:** **Shadow Partner FIRST.** It's the single
  biggest damage multiplier in the Assassin lineage. Max it before
  anything else.
- **Prep note:** NL stacks claw + star upgrades. Hwabi/Ilbi stars
  are your late-game grind targets. See the
  [Assassin build guide](/assassin-build) for star-level breakdowns.

### Chief Bandit (from Bandit)

**Role:** meso machine with Band of Thieves + Meso Explosion future.

- **[CONFIRMED]** Chief Bandit unlocks from Bandit at Lv 70.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Core kit: Band of Thieves (summon shadow bandits
  for AoE), Meso Guard (preserve mesos on death), Shield Mastery,
  Pickpocket (earn mesos from attacks).
- **[HELD]** Pickpocket meso-per-hit rate under CW's rebalanced
  NPC-sell economy. Band of Thieves exact AoE radius.
- **Day 1 priority:** Band of Thieves → Pickpocket. BoT is your
  training tool; PP builds the meso bank Chief Bandit lives for.
- **Prep note:** CB → Shadower → Meso Explosion is the long game.
  Meso Explosion isn't a 3rd-job skill (it's 4th-job Shadower),
  but Pickpocket feeds the piggy bank from day one.

<h2 id="grand-launch-day-plan">Grand Launch Day 1 plan</h2>

What to actually do on Oct 21, 2026:

### Hour 1: Advance to 3rd job

1. Hit the 3rd-job advancement NPC (location TBD per Nexon's launch
   patch notes). Expect the quest to send you to a boss arena for a
   trial — this is the long-standing MapleStory pattern.
2. Dump 1-2 points into your Day-1-priority skill (see table above).
3. Return to town.

### Hour 2-6: Hit a Priest or get Hyper Body

Priests with Holy Symbol give +~50% party EXP. If you're not a Priest,
find one. If you ARE a Priest, max Holy Symbol first and announce in
chat.

### Day 1-3: Grind L70-80 content with new skills

The new skills double your clear speed overnight. Grinding feels fresh.

### Day 4-7: Zakum ready-check

If Nexon opens Zakum Day 1 of Grand Launch (not yet confirmed), start
preparing a 6-player party:

- At least 2 Clerics/Priests (for Heal/Resurrection)
- 1-2 Dark Knights (Hyper Body carry)
- 2-3 DPS (NL, Hero, Mage mix)
- A tank with Power Guard (Paladin or Fighter)

Zakum fights take 30-60 minutes with a prepped party. The helmet drop
is the biggest single-item power jump in the game.

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

**Can I 4th-job in FA?**

No. 4th job unlocks at Lv 120 traditionally; neither 3rd nor 4th
advancement is live during FA. 3rd job opens at Grand Launch (Oct 21);
4th job timing is undisclosed by Nexon but historically landed 1-2
patches after a server launched.

**I'm Lv 65 with 3 days left in FA. Can I hit 100?**

No. Lv 65 → 100 is 70-100 hours of play past Lv 70; even at 10
hours/day that's a full week, and we have 3 days. Realistic aim
for the final sprint: push to Lv 75-80 to bank ~15-30 SP for
3rd job Day 1 skill priority, and spend the rest of your time
on the [done-with-FA checklist](/grand-launch-prep#done-with-fa-checklist)
(weapon scrolls, meso bank, earring crits). The Lv 65 → 70 push
matters more than Lv 70 → 100 - you unlock 3rd-job advancement at
Lv 70 which was the real gate anyway.

**Will my FA gear be obsolete at Grand Launch?**

No — your Lv 50-70 scrolled weapon and gloves carry straight into 3rd
job and remain competitive until Lv 90+. The thing that WILL get
upgraded quickly is your overall (Zakum Helmet replaces your Lv 60
helm) and your hat if Zakum opens Day 1.

**What's the single biggest Day-1 priority?**

For every class: **advance 3rd job and dump 1-2 points into your
Day-1-priority skill from the table above**. For the server broadly:
**find a Priest or BE the Priest.** Holy Symbol compounds all party
EXP for the week after launch.

**Is there a Lv 100 cap in FA?**

The FA event window ends Oct 20, so your effective cap is "whatever
you reach by then." The game itself doesn't hard-cap at Lv 100 —
you can keep going into 3rd job territory if the servers stay up,
but 3rd-job advancement is gated at Grand Launch.

**Can my FA character transfer to Grand Launch?**

Yes — Nexon has confirmed character carryover. Your Lv 70+ FA
character is the one you'll be 3rd-jobbing on Oct 21. Everything you
grind during FA counts.

**What about new classes (Pirate, Cygnus Knights, Hero Explorers)?**

Not in FA. Pirate class existed in traditional MapleStory from a later
patch and has not been confirmed for Grand Launch. Cygnus Knights and
Hero Explorers are post-Big-Bang content and are definitively NOT part
of Classic World's launch roster — Classic World is pre-Big-Bang by
design.
