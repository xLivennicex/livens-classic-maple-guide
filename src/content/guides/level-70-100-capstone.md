<h2 id="the-reality">The Lv. 70+ reality</h2>

From the Grand Launch release notes:

- **Maximum level is Lv. 100.** The only job advancements are at Lv. 10
  and Lv. 30. There is **no 3rd Job at Grand Launch**, and Nexon hasn't
  given it a date - Inkwell's Oct 2 interview said 3rd Job and some
  regions arrive sequentially after launch.
- **The world is Maple Island and Victoria Island**, plus the new
  **Excavation Site** in Perion. Ossyria, Zakum, and Papulatus are not
  part of Grand Launch.
- **Bosses:** the six launch bosses are [Mano](/bosses/mano), [Stumpy](/bosses/stumpy),
  [Mushmom](/bosses/mushmom), [Shade](/bosses/shade),
  [Zombie Mushmom](/bosses/zombie-mushmom), and [Jr. Balrog](/bosses/jr-balrog).
  Past Lv. 70 they are mostly drop runs - except [Jr. Balrog](/bosses/jr-balrog),
  who is Lv 80 with 325,400 HP in the Classic World client: the real
  endgame boss of Grand Launch.
- **Forgotten Hollow** opens in November - a likely new training option
  for this level range once it's live.

So Lv. 70-100 is a long grind on the maps you already know. That isn't
wasted time: every level is still HP, MP, AP, and SP for your 2nd job.

<h2 id="what-to-do">What to do with 30 levels</h2>

### 1. Finish your 2nd-job skills

Max your core 2nd-job skills first - they are your whole kit until 3rd
Job exists. See your branch in the [Class Builds hub](/class-builds).

### 2. Scroll your weapon and gloves

The biggest damage-per-meso lever. See the
[gearing guide](/gearing-30-plus#scrolling-philosophy) for the scroll math.
With no new gear tiers on the horizon, a well-scrolled weapon stays your
weapon for a long time.

### 3. Scroll your earrings

Classic World gives earrings a crit-damage line, which makes them a real
damage slot - see
[Classic World ground rules](/gearing-30-plus#classic-world-ground-rules).

### 4. Try the Excavation Site

Perion's new area is the one piece of genuinely new map content at
launch, and its far end is built for this level range: Officer
Skeletons (Lv 63) in the camps and **Commander Skeletons (Lv 73)** in
Remains &lt;Tomb&gt; IV and Remains &lt;Cliff&gt;. All undead and weak
to Holy. See the [Excavation Site guide](/excavation-site#cliff).

### 5. Catch up on quests

If you skipped them on the way up, Lv. 70+ is the time:

- [Henesys Community Board](/quests/henesys-community-board) - also feeds
  [Citizenship](/citizenship) grade, which now gates
  [Housing](/grand-launch-prep#systems).
- [Alex's Request](/quests/alexs-request)
- [Cursed Doll chain](/quests/cursed-doll-collection-chain)
- [Sauna Robe](/quests/sleepywood-sauna-robe-chain)

### 6. Play the launch events

The [Grand Launch Event Calendar](/launch-events) runs through November
18 (Mark of the Beta needs only Lv. 25), with GM events on Oct 22, Nov 6,
and Dec 19.

<h2 id="sp-question">Should you save SP for 3rd Job?</h2>

In traditional MapleStory you could hold unspent SP and spend it after a
later advancement. Whether Classic World works the same way hasn't been
confirmed. Even if it does, the trade-off has changed:

- **3rd Job has no date.** Holding SP means playing weaker for weeks or
  months on the chance of a head start later.
- **Your 2nd-job skills are your entire kit until then.** Unmaxed
  2nd-job skills cost you EXP and mesos every hour you play.

**Our recommendation:** max the 2nd-job skills you actually use. Only
hold SP you genuinely have no use for, and revisit when Nexon announces
3rd Job.

Secrets at a GM event.

<h2 id="when-3rd-job-arrives">When 3rd Job arrives</h2>

Nothing about Classic World's 3rd Job is confirmed - not the level, the
quest, the NPCs, or the skills. For orientation only, this is how the
2nd-job branches advanced in **traditional** MapleStory:

| 2nd Job | Traditional 3rd Job | Traditional 4th Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fighter | Crusader | Hero |
| Page | White Knight | Paladin |
| Spearman | Dragon Knight | Dark Knight |
| Wizard (Fire/Poison) | Mage (Fire/Poison) | Arch Mage (Fire/Poison) |
| Wizard (Ice/Lightning) | Mage (Ice/Lightning) | Arch Mage (Ice/Lightning) |
| Cleric | Priest | Bishop |
| Hunter | Ranger | Bowmaster |
| Crossbowman | Sniper | Marksman |
| Assassin | Hermit | Night Lord |
| Bandit | Chief Bandit | Shadower |

Inkwell has said Classic World is not a 1:1 recreation and that content
won't follow the original release order, so treat this as history, not
a forecast. We'll replace this table with real details when Nexon
publishes them.

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

**Is there a level cap?**

Yes - **Lv. 100**, at Founder's Access and at Grand Launch.

**Does 3rd Job open at Grand Launch?**

No. Only the Lv. 10 and Lv. 30 advancements exist at launch, and 3rd Job
has no announced date.

**Can I fight Zakum?**

Not at Grand Launch. The six launch bosses are all on Victoria Island.

**Will my Founder's Access character carry over?**

Yes - Nexon confirmed Founder's Access progress carries into Grand Launch.

**What about Pirates, Cygnus Knights, or other later classes?**

The Grand Launch release notes list four jobs: Warrior, Magician, Bowman,
and Thief. Nothing else has been announced.
