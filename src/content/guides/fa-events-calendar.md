<h2 id="the-four-events">The four events</h2>

All four events are part of the **Physical Fitness Test** system
Nexon added to Classic World (see
[What's New in Classic World](/guides/whats-new-in-cot2#physical-fitness-test-the-new-event-jq-system)).
They're hosted in a dedicated Event Hall map with per-level rooms,
and GMs toggle them on/off at scheduled times.

The common structure:

- You enter the event through the Event Hall NPC in your current town.
- The event instance is **level-gated** - you join a room matching
  your level bracket.
- Rewards are **mailed on completion** or dropped as items in the
  instance map.
- All rewards **persist into Grand Launch** - nothing is FA-only in
  the sense of expiring.

<h2 id="physical-fitness-challenge">Physical Fitness Challenge</h2>

**Dates:** Oct 7 (past), Oct 14 (past). **No further runs scheduled.**
**Type:** Jump-quest obstacle course
**Format:** solo instance, timed completion

### What it is

A multi-stage jump quest in a dedicated Event Hall instance.
Platforms, moving obstacles, and timed sections. Completion rewards
scale with how fast you finish.

### Rewards

Specific reward values haven't been published by Nexon. Based on
the Physical Fitness Test system's structure (per
[What's New in Classic World](/guides/whats-new-in-cot2#physical-fitness-test-the-new-event-jq-system)):

- **Experience** on completion, likely scaled to your level bracket.
- **Reward items** from the Event Hall redemption NPC - specifics
  unconfirmed for CW; traditional MapleStory versions of this event
  type give jump potions and consumables.
- **Possible crate rolls** - some Oct 7 attendees reported crate
  pulls but Nexon hasn't confirmed these as scheduled rewards. Treat
  as upside, not a guarantee.

### Who should attend

- **Lv 1-50**: yes, free level
- **Lv 50-70**: yes but prioritize if you have Lv 1-50 alts
- **Lv 70+**: optional - the EXP burst is thin relative to grinding
  time, but the jump potions are useful consumables and the ~15
  minute time commitment is low

### Prep notes

Bring Teleport Rocks or a Magician alt - the course has sections
where re-entry from the start is punishing on time.

<h2 id="coconut-harvest">Coconut Harvest</h2>

**Dates:** Oct 12 (past, one-shot)
**Type:** Team gathering mini-game
**Status:** This event has already run and is NOT scheduled to repeat
in FA. We're documenting it here for completeness and in case Nexon
adds it to the Grand Launch rotation.

### What it was

A Florina Beach map transformed into a coconut-tree-dense event
instance. Teams raced to shake coconuts from palm trees and deliver
them to a central NPC. First-team-to-N wins the top-tier reward tier.

### Rewards (community-reported, unverified by Nexon)

- **Coconut items** - redeemable at the event NPC for the top tier
  of rewards (team-first), diminishing for lower placements.
- **Fashion-crate related items** - community reports vary; no
  official reward list has been published.
- **GM-handed cosmetics** - unverified.

### Was it worth attending

Mid-tier. Fun social event, but the EXP was weak and the fashion-crate
redemption pool was random. If you missed it, you didn't miss a
character-defining reward.

<h2 id="up-and-up">Up and Up</h2>

**Dates:** Oct 9 (past), **Oct 19 (upcoming - final run)**
**Type:** Platform-climbing race
**Format:** multi-player instance, elimination format

### What it is

A tower of ever-higher platforms. Players race to the top; falling
drops you back a stage. Last-player-standing or first-to-top wins.
Think of it as MapleStory's version of Fall Guys' tower stage.

### Rewards

Official reward table not published. Community reports after the
Oct 9 run mention:

- **EXP** scaled to final placement
- **Consumables** from the Event Hall redemption NPC
- **Scroll of Secrets** - rare drop, community-reported from Oct 9
  but not confirmed as a scheduled reward. Trades at a premium on
  the Free Market when it does drop.

### Who should attend

- **Any level**: Up and Up is a social event with meaningful rewards
  at every tier. Even if you don't win, finishing gets you consumables.
- **Priority target** for Lv 30-70 players who want cheap jump
  potions for Forgotten Hollow runs.

### Prep notes

- **Don't bring a laggy connection.** Platform events are tick-sensitive.
- Historical versions of this event ban certain mobility skills
  (Teleport, Haste) to keep the race fair - unverified for CW's
  current implementation. Not a practical concern in FA since 3rd
  job is gated anyway.

<h2 id="monster-invasion">Monster Invasion</h2>

**Dates:** Oct 11 (past), Oct 16 (past), **Oct 20 (upcoming - final day of FA)**
**Type:** Mass mob spawn in a town
**Format:** open-world, all players in the invaded town participate

### What it is

A GM spawns waves of mobs in a specific town (Henesys / Perion /
Ellinia / Kerning on rotation). Players kill mobs for burst EXP
and crate drops. The invasion lasts 15-30 minutes per announcement.

### Rewards (community-reported from Oct 11's run)

- **EXP** - community consensus is this is the biggest EXP burst
  of the four events. Mobs appear to be tuned to give boosted EXP
  for the duration (not independently verified vs their normal
  EXP values).
- **Fashion-crate drops** - Oct 11 attendees reported Aurora /
  Founder's crate items dropping from Invasion mobs. Drop rate
  unknown.
- **Scroll of Secrets** - reported from Oct 11 but not confirmed
  by Nexon as a scheduled drop.

### Who should attend

**Everyone**, every time. Monster Invasion is the top-value FA event
by a wide margin. The EXP alone is worth the time investment, and
the crate-drop lottery is a free pull per attendance.

### Prep notes

- **Pre-position** - sit at the invasion town's spawn plaza with
  consumables stocked. The event starts with limited advance warning
  (community reports say 2-5 minutes).
- **Party up** - party XP (+170% in a 6-player party per the FA
  client values) stacks with Invasion EXP for the biggest multiplier
  in the game right now.
- The Oct 20 Invasion falls on the final day of FA. Whether Nexon
  boosts the last-day iteration is historically common in GMS events
  but unconfirmed for this run specifically.
- **Attack-spell classes clear faster than ranged** - F/P Mage with
  Poison Breath + Explosion is the top Invasion class in FA
  (3rd job's Explosion isn't available yet, so 2nd-job Fire Arrow
  is the ceiling).

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

**Where does the event start - which NPC do I talk to?**

In the running client, every town's Event Hall NPC appears during
event windows. The current build routes you to the Event Hall map
(IDs in the 109xxx range per the Nexon map datamine). Watch the
in-game event ticker for the exact entry point - it announces
"Event is now open - speak with [NPC name] at [location]."

**Do I need to be a specific level to attend?**

Most events gate by level bracket (Lv 1-30, 30-50, 50-70). You can
attend any bracket you qualify for, but higher brackets usually have
better rewards and more competitive players. Lv 70+ players should
queue for the highest bracket they qualify for (the FA cap was Lv. 100).

**Will events repeat during Grand Launch?**

**Unknown.** Nexon's Oct 3 release notes list the four events as
"Founder's Access events" but don't specify Grand Launch rotation.
Community sentiment is that at least Monster Invasion and Up and Up
will continue post-launch as recurring GM events, but no official
schedule has been published.

**Can I attend events on multiple characters?**

**Yes.** FA characters are per-account unlimited (there's no
character cap during FA per Nexon's release notes). If you have
multiple alts, you can rotate them through events for multiplied
rewards. The server doesn't tag your main-vs-alt.

**What if a scheduled event doesn't fire?**

Nexon has pushed events by hours in historical GMS events. Watch the
official Discord's `#announcements` channel for live reschedule
notices. If an event date passes without the event firing, it's
typically rescheduled within 24-48 hours.

**Are events region-locked?**

**No.** FA is global on a single server cluster per Nexon's release
notes. Any character on the FA server can join any event instance.
Timezone-wise, 11:00 AM Pacific = 2 PM Eastern = 8 PM CEST = 4 AM
AEST (next day).

**Can I use the Mu Lung Dojo / Omega Sector / other event systems?**

**No - those are gated.** Only the Physical Fitness Test system is
live in FA. Mu Lung Dojo, Omega Sector, and the other repeatable
event dungeons open at Grand Launch (Oct 21) per Nexon's release
notes. See [FA Content Access Status](/fa-access-status#confirmed-gated).
